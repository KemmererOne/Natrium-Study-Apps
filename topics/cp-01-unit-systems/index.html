Fundamental dimensions

Objectives 1.1

"Length, mass, and time are the three fundamental dimensions on which the measurement of all physical quantities is dependent." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.1

Mass describes how much material makes up an object. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.1

"Mass is the amount of material present in an object." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.1

Mass and weight are often confused because the units used to describe them are similar. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.1

"Weight (a derived unit, not a fundamental unit) is a measurement that describes the force of gravity on the "mass" of an object." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.1
"Length is the distance between two points." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.1
"Time is the duration between two instants." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.2

The concept of length is needed to locate the position of a point in space and to describe the size of a physical object or system. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.1

Time is described in units of seconds, minutes, or hours. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.2

Why is weight not one of the three fundamental dimensions?

The answer is B. The handbook calls weight a derived unit, not a fundamental unit. It describes the force of gravity on the mass of an object. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.1

Unit systems

Objectives 1.2

"A number alone is not sufficient to describe a physical quantity." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.2

The unit defines the magnitude of a measurement, and the same physical quantity may be measured using a variety of different units. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.2

"There are two unit systems in use at the present time, English units and International System of Units (SI)." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.2

The English system is based on three standard units: DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.8

  • Foot
  • Pound
  • Second

DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.8

"The foot-pound-second (FPS) system is the usual unit system used in the U.S. when dealing with physics." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.3
"The SI system is made up of two related systems, the meter-kilogram-second (MKS) system and the centimeter-gram-second (CGS) system." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.3

The MKS standard units are the meter, kilogram, and second. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.8

The CGS standard units are the centimeter, gram, and second. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.8

MKS and CGS use a decimal-based system in which prefixes denote powers of ten. One kilometer is 1000 meters, and one centimeter is one one-hundredth of a meter. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.3

The English system has odd units of conversion. A mile is 5280 feet, and an inch is one twelfth of a foot. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.3

Fundamental and derived measurements

Objectives 1.3

Most physical quantities have units that are combinations of the three fundamental dimensions of length, mass, and time. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.6

"When these dimensions or measurements are combined, they produce what are referred to as derived units." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.6

Area is the product of two lengths, so it has units of length squared, such as in.² or m². DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.6

Volume is the product of three lengths, so it has units of length cubed, such as in.³ or m³. One liter equals 1000 cm³. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.6

"Density is a measure of the mass of an object per unit volume" DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.7

Density has units of mass divided by length cubed, such as kg/m³ or lbs/ft³. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.7

Velocity is the change in length per unit time, with units such as km/h or ft/s. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.7

Acceleration is the change in velocity per unit time, with units such as cm/s² or ft/s². DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.7

Unit conversion

Objectives 1.4, 1.5, 1.6

"Conversion factors are based on relationships of equivalents from different measurement systems." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.9

Conversion tables define the equivalent relationships between different units of measurement. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.9

"Conversion is basically a multiplication by 1." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.10

Common length equivalents: 1 yd = 0.9144 m, 12 in. = 1 ft, 5280 ft = 1 mile, 1 m = 3.281 ft, and 1 in. = 0.0254 m. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.10

Common mass equivalents: 1 lbm = 0.4535 kg, 2.205 lbm = 1 kg, and 1 kg = 1000 g. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.10

Common time equivalents: 60 sec = 1 min and 3600 sec = 1 hr. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.10

Longer time equivalents: 1 day = 86,400 seconds = 1440 minutes = 24 hours, and 1 year = 8760 hours = 365 days. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.15

The conversion takes three steps: DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.16

  • Select the equivalent relationship from the conversion table.
  • Express the relationship as a conversion factor.
  • Multiply the given quantity by the conversion factor.

DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.16

"If an equivalent relationship between the given units and the desired units cannot be found in the conversion tables, multiple conversion factors must be used." DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.12

Worked examples

Objective 1.4

Feet to inches

Convert 5 feet to inches.

  1. Select the relationship: 1 ft = 12 inches.
  2. Express it as a factor equal to 1: 12 inches / 1 foot.
  3. Multiply: 5 feet x 12 inches/foot = 5 x 12 inches.

Answer: 60 inches

DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.11

Objective 1.4

Meters to feet

Convert 795 m to ft.

  1. Select the relationship: 1 meter = 3.281 ft.
  2. Divide to get the ratio desired units / present units: 3.281 ft / 1 m.
  3. Multiply: 795 m x 3.281 ft/m = 795 x 3.281 ft.

Answer: 2608.395 ft

DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.11

Objective 1.4

Multiple conversion factors

Convert 2.91 sq miles to sq meters.

  1. No direct conversion is in the table, so go from sq miles to sq yd to sq ft to sq m.
  2. (2.91 sq miles) x (3.098 x 10⁶ sq yd / 1 sq mile) = 9.015 x 10⁶ sq yd.
  3. (9.015 x 10⁶ sq yd) x (9 sq ft / 1 sq yd) = 8.114 x 10⁷ sq ft.
  4. (8.114 x 10⁷ sq ft) x (1 sq m / 10.764 sq ft).

Answer: 7.538 x 10⁶ sq m

DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.13

Objective 1.4

Valve dimensions in MKS units

A valve stem is 57.20 in. long. The valve inlet and outlet are 22.00 in. I.D. and 27.50 in. O.D. List these in the MKS system.

  1. Stem: 57.20 in. x 0.0254 m/in. = 1.453 m.
  2. I.D.: 22.00 x 0.0254 = 0.559 m.
  3. O.D.: 27.50 x 0.0254 = 0.699 m.

Answer: 1.453 m stem, 0.559 m I.D., 0.699 m O.D.

DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.13

Summary

  • Length is the distance between two points, mass is the amount of material in an object, and time is the duration between two instants. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.8
  • The English system is based on the foot, pound, and second. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.8
  • The SI system uses the meter, kilogram, and second (MKS), or the centimeter, gram, and second (CGS). DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.8
  • Derived units combine units to describe quantities such as area, volume, and density. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.8
  • Conversion tables list equivalent relationships, and conversion factors are obtained by dividing to get a multiplying factor of 1. DOE-HDBK-1010-92 CP-01 p.16

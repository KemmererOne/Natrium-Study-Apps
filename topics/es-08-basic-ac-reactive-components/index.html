DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92, Module 8.

Terminal objective

1.0 Using the rules associated with inductors and capacitors, DESCRIBE the characteristics of these elements when they are placed in an AC circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

Enabling objectives

1.1 DESCRIBE inductive reactance (XL). DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.2 Given the operation frequency (f) and the value of inductance (L), CALCULATE the inductive reactance (XL) of a simple circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.3 DESCRIBE the effect of the phase relationship between current and voltage in an inductive circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.4 DRAW a simple phasor diagram representing AC current (I) and voltage (E) in an inductive circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.5 DEFINE capacitive reactance (XC). DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.6 Given the operating frequency (f) and the value of capacitance (C), CALCULATE the capacitive reactance (XC) of a simple AC circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.7 DESCRIBE the effect on phase relationship between current (I) and voltage (E) in a capacitive circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.8 DRAW a simple phasor diagram representing AC current (I) and voltage (E) in a capacitive circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.9 DEFINE impedance (Z). DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.10 Given the values for resistance (R) and inductance (L) and a simple R-L series AC circuit, CALCULATE the impedance (Z) for that circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.11 Given the values for resistance (R) and capacitance (C) and a simple R-C series AC circuit, CALCULATE the impedance (Z) for that circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.12 Given a simple R-C-L series AC circuit and the values for resistance (R), inductive reactance (XL), and capacitive reactance (XC), CALCULATE the impedance (Z) for that circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.v

1.13 STATE the formula for calculating total current (IT) in a simple parallel R-C-L AC circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.vi

1.14 Given a simple R-C-L parallel AC circuit and the values for voltage (VT), resistance (R), inductive reactance (XL), and capacitive reactance (XC), CALCULATE the impedance (Z) for that circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.vi

1.15 DEFINE resonance. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.vi

1.16 Given the values of capacitance (C) and inductance (L), CALCULATE the resonant frequency. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.vi

1.17 Given a series R-C-L circuit at resonance, DESCRIBE the net reactance of the circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.vi

1.18 Given a parallel R-C-L circuit at resonance, DESCRIBE the circuit output relative to current (I). DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.vi

Inductive reactance

Objectives 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4

In an inductive AC circuit, the current is continually changing and continuously inducing an EMF that opposes the change. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.1

"This opposition of the inductance to the flow of an alternating current is called inductive reactance (XL)." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.1

XL depends on the inductance of the circuit and on the frequency of the applied voltage. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.1

XL = 2πfL DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.4

I = E / XL DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.1

The self-induced EMF varies as a sine wave and lags the current by 90°. The applied voltage must be equal and opposite to it at all times. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.3

"the current lags the applied voltage by 90° in a purely inductive circuit." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.3

If the applied voltage is a vector rotating counterclockwise, the current is a vector lagging it by 90°. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.3

"Diagrams of this type are referred to as phasor diagrams." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.3

Knowledge check: Inductive reactance

Objective 1.1Q716

What is inductive reactance?

The answer is A. The opposition of the inductance to the flow of an alternating current is called inductive reactance (XL). DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.1

Report a problem
Objective 1.2Q717

What is the inductive reactance of a 0.1 henry coil at 60 Hz?

The answer is C. XL = 2πfL = (2)(3.14)(60)(0.1), which is about 37.7 ohms. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.1

Report a problem
Objective 1.3Q427

In a purely inductive circuit, current:

The answer is C. Current lags applied voltage in a purely inductive circuit by a 90 degree phase angle. DOE-1011-V3 (DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92), Inductance, ES-08 p. 4

Report a problem
Objective 1.4Q718

On a phasor diagram of a purely inductive circuit, where is the current vector?

The answer is B. The current can be expressed as a vector that is lagging the applied voltage by 90°. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.3

Capacitive reactance

Objectives 1.5, 1.6, 1.7, 1.8

The current flow in a circuit containing capacitance depends on the rate at which the voltage changes. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.5

"In any purely capacitive circuit, current leads applied voltage by 90°." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.6
"Capacitive reactance is the opposition by a capacitor or a capacitive circuit to the flow of current." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.6
"capacitive reactance, which is the opposition to current flow, is inversely proportional to frequency and capacitance." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.6

Capacitive reactance is measured in ohms, as is inductive reactance. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.6

XC = 1 / (2πfC) DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.8

I = E / XC DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.7

The phasor diagram shows current leading voltage by 90°. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.8

Knowledge check: Capacitive reactance

Objective 1.5Q719

If frequency increases, what happens to capacitive reactance?

The answer is B. Capacitive reactance is inversely proportional to frequency and capacitance. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.6

Objective 1.6Q720

What is the capacitive reactance of a 100 µF capacitor at 60 Hz?

The answer is C. XC = 1 / (2πfC) = 1 / [(2)(3.14)(60)(100 x 10⁻⁶)], which is about 26.5 ohms. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.6

Objective 1.7Q721

What is the phase relationship in a purely capacitive circuit?

The answer is D. In any purely capacitive circuit, current leads applied voltage by 90°. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.6

Report a problem
Objective 1.8Q722

On a phasor diagram of a purely capacitive circuit, the current vector:

The answer is A. The phasor diagram shows current leading voltage by 90°. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.6

Impedance

Objectives 1.9, 1.10, 1.11, 1.12, 1.13, 1.14

"Impedance is defined as the total opposition to current flow in a circuit." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.9

Z = √(R² + X²) DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.10

"Net reactance in an AC circuit is the difference between inductive and capacitive reactance." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.10
"Impedance in an AC circuit corresponds to the resistance of a DC circuit." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.11

V = IZ DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.11

"Impedance is the resultant of phasor addition of R and XL." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.11

Z = √(R² + XL²) DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.11

"impedance is the resultant of phasor addition of R and XC." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.12

Z = √(R² + XC²) DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.12

"Impedance in an R-C-L series circuit is equal to the phasor sum of resistance, inductive reactance, and capacitive reactance" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.13

Z = √(R² + (XL - XC)²) DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.14

Because the difference between XL and XC is squared, the order of subtraction does not affect the answer. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.14

"Impedance in a parallel R-C-L circuit equals the voltage divided by the total current." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.15

ZT = VT / IT DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.15

"Total current in a parallel R-C-L circuit is equal to the square root of the sum of the squares of the current flows through the resistance, inductive reactance, and capacitive reactance branches of the circuit." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.16

IT = √(IR² + (IC - IL)²) DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.16

Knowledge check: Impedance

Objective 1.9Q723

How does the handbook define impedance?

The answer is C. Impedance is defined as the total opposition to current flow in a circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.9

Report a problem
Objective 1.10Q724

A series R-L circuit has R = 30 ohms and XL = 40 ohms. What is the impedance?

The answer is B. Z = √(30² + 40²) = √2500 = 50 ohms. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.11

Report a problem
Objective 1.11Q725

A series R-C circuit has R = 60 ohms and XC = 80 ohms. What is the impedance?

The answer is C. Z = √(60² + 80²) = √10,000 = 100 ohms. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.12

Report a problem
Objective 1.12Q726

A series R-C-L circuit has R = 3 ohms, XL = 10 ohms, and XC = 6 ohms. What is the impedance?

The answer is A. Z = √(3² + (10 - 6)²) = √25 = 5 ohms. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.13

Report a problem
Objective 1.13Q727

Which formula gives total current in a parallel R-C-L circuit?

The answer is B. Total current is the square root of the sum of the squares: IT = √(IR² + (IC - IL)²). DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.16

Report a problem
Objective 1.14Q728

A parallel R-C-L circuit has 100 volts applied, with IR = 3 A, IL = 6 A, and IC = 2 A. What is the impedance?

The answer is C. IT = √(3² + (6 - 2)²) = 5 A, and Z = VT / IT = 100 / 5 = 20 ohms. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.15

Resonance

Objectives 1.15, 1.16, 1.17, 1.18

"Resonance occurs in an AC circuit when inductive reactance and capacitive reactance are equal to one another" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.19

When XL = XC, the total reactance becomes zero and the impedance is totally resistive. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.19

fRes = 1 / (2π√(LC)) DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.19

"at resonance the net reactance of the circuit is zero, and the impedance is equal to the circuit resistance" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.19

The current output of a series resonant circuit is at a maximum value for that circuit and is determined by the value of the resistance. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.19

In a parallel R-C-L circuit at resonance, the inductive and capacitive currents are equal and opposite in phase, so they cancel one another. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.20

"the net current flow through the circuit is at minimum because of the high impendence presented by XL and XC in parallel." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.20

Knowledge check: Resonance

Objective 1.15Q729

When does resonance occur in an AC circuit?

The answer is C. Resonance occurs in an AC circuit when inductive reactance and capacitive reactance are equal to one another. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.19

Report a problem
Objective 1.16Q730

A circuit has L = 0.1 henry and C = 10 µF. What is the resonant frequency?

The answer is C. fRes = 1 / (2π√(LC)) = 1 / [(2)(3.14)(1 x 10⁻³)], which is about 159 Hz. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.19

Report a problem
Objective 1.17Q731

What is the net reactance of a series R-C-L circuit at resonance?

The answer is A. At resonance the net reactance of the circuit is zero, and the impedance is equal to the circuit resistance. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.19

Report a problem
Objective 1.18Q732

What is the net current flow through a parallel R-C-L circuit at resonance?

The answer is B. The net current flow through the circuit is at minimum because of the high impedance presented by XL and XC in parallel. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.20

Worked examples

Objective 1.2

Inductive circuit

A 0.4 H coil with negligible resistance is connected to a 115 V, 60 Hz source. Find the inductive reactance and the current.

  1. XL = 2πfL = (2)(3.14)(60)(0.4) = 150.7 Ω.
  2. I = E / XL = 115 / 150.7.

Answer: 150.7 Ω and 0.76 amps, with current lagging voltage by 90°

DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.4

Objective 1.6

Capacitive circuit

A 10 µF capacitor is connected to a 120 V, 60 Hz source. Find the current if the capacitive reactance is 265.4 Ω.

  1. I = E / XC.
  2. I = 120 / 265.4.

Answer: 0.452 amps, with current leading voltage by 90°

DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.8

Objective 1.10

R-L impedance

A 100 Ω resistor and a 60 Ω XL are in series. What is the circuit impedance?

  1. Z = √(R² + XL²).
  2. Z = √(100² + 60²) = √13,600.

Answer: 116.6 Ω

DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.12

Objective 1.12

R-C-L impedance

Find the impedance of a series R-C-L circuit when R = 6 Ω, XL = 20 Ω, and XC = 10 Ω.

  1. Z = √(R² + (XL - XC)²).
  2. Z = √(6² + (20 - 10)²) = √136.

Answer: 11.66 Ω

DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.15

Objective 1.14

Parallel R-C-L circuit

A 200 Ω resistor, a 100 Ω XL, and an 80 Ω XC are in parallel across a 120 V source. Find the total current and impedance.

  1. Branch currents: IR = 120/200 = 0.6 A, IL = 120/100 = 1.2 A, IC = 120/80 = 1.5 A.
  2. IT = √((0.6)² + (1.5 - 1.2)²) = √0.45 = 0.671 A.
  3. Z = 120 / 0.671.

Answer: 0.671 A and 178.8 Ω

DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.17

Summary

  • Opposition to the flow of alternating current caused by inductance is inductive reactance, and current lags applied voltage by 90° in a purely inductive circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.4
  • Opposition caused by capacitance is capacitive reactance, and current leads applied voltage by 90° in a purely capacitive circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.8
  • Impedance is the total opposition to current flow in an AC circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.18
  • Resonance is a state in which the inductive reactance equals the capacitive reactance at a specified frequency. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.20
  • At resonance, a series R-C-L circuit has zero net reactance, and a parallel R-C-L circuit has minimum current output. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-08 p.20

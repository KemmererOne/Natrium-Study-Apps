Why sodium leaks must be detected

From the cited sources

Sodium leak detection is important for three reasons: NRC slides ML19149A378, slide 25

"Primary sodium is radioactive" NRC slides ML19149A378, slide 25
"Liquid sodium will burn in air" NRC slides ML19149A378, slide 25
"Loss of sodium could impair heat transport systems" NRC slides ML19149A378, slide 25

Sodium systems run at low pressure, so under the leak before break approach: NRC slides ML19149A433, slide 21

"Due to low pressure, sodium leaks are generally small and can be detected before larger piping breaks occur" NRC slides ML19149A433, slide 21
"Detection of small leaks helps prevent large leaks from occurring." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 8.2.2, p. 8.2-4

Types of sodium leak detectors

From the cited sources

NRC training slides list these ways of detecting sodium leaks: NRC slides ML19149A433, slide 21

  • Smoke detectors
  • Contact detectors (spark plugs)
  • Cable detectors
  • Aerosol monitors
  • Inspections

NRC slides ML19149A433, slide 21

"Leaks can be detected by conductivity probes (usually in low spots below sodium-containing tanks) or by sensing of sodium aerosols" NRC slides ML19149A378, slide 25

Double-walled piping may also carry leak detectors in the gap between the walls. NRC slides ML19149A433, slide 21

Spark plug type detectors

From the cited sources

"spark plug type leak detectors function on the principal of fluid completing an open circuit." Word: Sodium Leak Detectors.docx, slide 1.5 Knowledge Check 1

So the detector's circuit is normally open, and leaking fluid closes it. The lesson plan's diagrams are pictures and are not used. Word: Sodium Leak Detectors.docx, slide 1.5 Knowledge Check 1

Leak detection at Natrium

From the cited sources

The Sodium Leak Detection, Collection, and Containment System (NNA): PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.1, p. 7.5-10

"detects, collects, and contains sodium leaks to mitigate sodium fires" PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.1, p. 7.5-10
"The specific type of sodium leak detector is based on the needs of the installation location and configuration." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16
"Sodium leak detectors are located within leak mitigative features, at vulnerable valves, and inside catch pans." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16
"Detectors will generally be based on conductivity detection with a self-test function." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16
"In areas where sodium reactions may occur (e.g., Intermediate Air Cooling stacks), smoke detection is provided." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16
"The sodium leak detectors activate an alarm within the MCR upon detection of sodium." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16
"Leak detection is provided at multiple points between pipe supports along all normally sodium containing systems" PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 8.2.1.8, p. 8.2-3
"The leak detectors are capable of detecting leaks within the entire length of pipe it covers." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 8.2.1.8, p. 8.2-3
"SPS leak detection uses devices such as wire and cable, spark plugs, and mutual inductance type sensors." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.4.2.2, p. 7.2-47

Sodium leakage from the reactor vessel into the guard vessel is watched through the cover gas system. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.2.1, p. 7.2-28

"Annulus gas is recirculated and monitored for sodium buildup via absolute filter differential pressure changes, oxygen concentrations, and radiation levels." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.2.1, p. 7.2-28

Q84

What is the design basis strategy of the Sodium Leak Detection, Collection, and Containment System (NNA)?

The answer is A. The PSAR states NNA mitigates sodium fires after a leak and that leak prevention is outside its scope; smoke detection and fire water distribution belong to other fire protection subsystems it describes. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.1, p. 7.5-10

What a leak signal does

From the cited sources

NNA leak detection signals support automatic actions for the primary Sodium Processing System (SPS-P): PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.2, p. 7.5-12

  • NNA sends a signal to the Auxiliary Monitoring and Control System (AMC), which automatically closes the SPS-P supply valve.
  • AMC automatically trips the SPS-P pumps.
  • NNA sends a signal to the Utility Monitoring and Control System (UMC), which automatically closes NI HVAC dampers to isolate the SPS cells.

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.2, p. 7.5-12

Summary

  • Leak detection matters because primary sodium is radioactive, sodium burns in air, and sodium loss could impair heat transport. NRC slides ML19149A378, slide 25
  • Detection methods include smoke, contact (spark plug), cable and aerosol detectors, and inspections. NRC slides ML19149A433, slide 21
  • In a spark plug type detector, leaking fluid completes an open circuit. Word: Sodium Leak Detectors.docx, slide 1.5 Knowledge Check 1
  • Natrium detectors will generally use conductivity detection with a self-test function, and they alarm in the main control room. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16
  • Detection of small leaks helps prevent large leaks from occurring. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 8.2.2, p. 8.2-4
  • NNA signals drive automatic SPS-P valve closure, pump trip and cell damper closure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.2, p. 7.5-12

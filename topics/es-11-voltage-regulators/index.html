Why voltage is regulated

Objectives 1.1

"The purpose of a voltage regulator is to maintain the output voltage of a generator at a desired value." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.1

As load on an AC generator changes, the voltage tends to change, mainly because of the changing voltage drop across the armature winding. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.1

The IR drop depends on the load change only. The IXL drop depends on the load change and also the power factor of the circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.1

"the output voltage of an AC generator varies with both changes in load (i.e., current) and changes in power factor." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.1

The regulator circuits

Objectives 1.2

"The sensing circuit senses output voltage of the AC generator." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.2

Its signal is proportional to output voltage and goes to the comparison circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.2

"The reference circuit maintains a constant output for reference." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.2

This reference is the desired voltage output of the AC generator. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.2

"The comparison circuit electrically compares the reference voltage to the sensed voltage and provides an error signal." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.2

The amplification circuit: DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.2

"takes the signal from the comparison circuit and amplifies the milliamp input to an amp output" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.2
"The signal output circuit, which controls field excitation of the AC generator, increases or decreases field excitation to either raise or lower the AC output voltage." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.3

The feedback circuit takes some of the output of the signal output circuit and feeds it back to the amplification circuit. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.3

"It does this to prevent overshooting or undershooting of the desired voltage by slowing down the circuit response." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.3

Knowledge check: The regulator circuits

Objective 1.2Q432

In a voltage regulator, which circuit compares reference voltage to output voltage?

The answer is D. The comparison circuit compares reference voltage to output voltage and provides an error signal to the amplification circuit. DOE-1011-V3 (DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92), Voltage Regulators, ES-11 p. 4

Objective 1.2Q761

Which voltage regulator circuit controls the field excitation of the AC generator?

The answer is C. The signal output circuit controls field excitation, increasing or decreasing it to raise or lower the AC output voltage. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.3

What does the feedback circuit prevent?

The answer is A. The feedback circuit prevents overshooting or undershooting of the desired voltage by slowing down the circuit response. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.3

Worked examples

Objective 1.2

Load increases

Generator load increases and output voltage drops. Follow the regulator's response.

  1. The sensing circuit senses the decrease and lowers its input to the comparison circuit.
  2. The reference stays constant, so the comparison circuit develops a positive error signal.
  3. The amplifier circuit amplifies it and sends it to the signal output circuit.
  4. The signal output circuit increases field excitation.

Answer: Generated voltage increases to the desired output

DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-11 p.3

Objective 1.2

Load decreases

Generator load decreases and output voltage rises. What does the regulator do?

  1. The comparison circuit develops a negative error signal.
  2. The signal output circuit decreases field excitation.

Answer: Generated voltage decreases to the desired output

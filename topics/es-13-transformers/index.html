Transformer terms and theory

Objectives 1.1

"The inducing of an EMF in a coil by magnetic flux lines generated in another coil is called mutual induction." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.1
"The turns ratio is defined as the ratio of turns of wire in the primary winding to the number of turns of wire in the secondary winding." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.2

Turns ratio = NP / NS DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.2

"Maximum power is transferred from one circuit to another through a transformer when the impedances are equal, or matched." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.2
"The ratio between the two impedances is referred to as the impedance ratio" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.2

The impedance ratio equals the square of the turns ratio, so a set turns ratio can match impedances. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.2

"Efficiency of a transformer is the ratio of the power output to the power input" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.3

Efficiency = (Power output / Power input) x 100 DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.3

Energy is transferred by magnetic induction from one set of coils to another by means of a varying magnetic flux. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.3

"The coil of a transformer that is energized from an AC source is called the primary winding (coil), and the coil that delivers this AC to the load is called the secondary winding (coil)" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.3
"The voltage of the windings in a transformer is directly proportional to the number of turns on the coils." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.5
"the voltage ratio is equal to the turns ratio." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.5

VP / VS = NP / NS DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.5

If the secondary voltage is greater than the primary voltage, the transformer is a step-up transformer. If it is less, it is a step-down transformer. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.5

"The current in the windings of a transformer is inversely proportional to the voltage in the windings." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.7

IS / IP = NP / NS DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.7

A transformer that steps up voltage steps down the current proportionally. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.8

All transformers have copper and core losses. Copper loss is power lost to the ohmic resistance of the windings. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.11

Core losses are caused by hysteresis and eddy current losses. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.12

Knowledge check: Transformer terms and theory

Objective 1.1Q775

What is mutual induction?

The answer is A. The inducing of an EMF in a coil by magnetic flux lines generated in another coil is called mutual induction. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.1

Report a problem
Objective 1.1Q776

A transformer has 1200 primary turns and 300 secondary turns, with 480 volts on the primary. What is the secondary voltage?

The answer is A. The voltage ratio equals the turns ratio: VS = 480 x 300/1200 = 120 volts. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.5

Report a problem
Objective 1.1Q777

When are impedances matched across a transformer most useful?

The answer is B. Maximum power is transferred from one circuit to another through a transformer when the impedances are equal, or matched. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.2

Report a problem
Objective 1.1Q778

A transformer takes in 5000 watts and delivers 4500 watts. What is its efficiency?

The answer is C. Efficiency = (4500 / 5000) x 100 = 90 percent. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.3

Three-phase connections

Objectives 1.2, 1.3

Three-phase transformer operation is identical to single-phase, except that three single-phase windings are used. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.8

"In the delta connection, all three phases are connected in series to form a closed loop" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.8
"In the wye connection, three common ends of each phase are connected together at a common terminal" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.8

The windings may be connected to form a three-phase bank in four ways: ∆-∆, ∆-Y, Y-∆, and Y-Y. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.9

With line voltage V and line current I, a delta primary winding has voltage V, and a wye primary winding has voltage V divided by √3. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.10

A delta primary winding has phase current I divided by √3, and a wye primary winding has phase current I. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.10

With turns ratio a, the secondary line current is aI for ∆-∆ and Y-Y, and √3 aI for Y-∆. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.11

Knowledge check: Three-phase connections

Objective 1.2Q779

How are the three phases of a delta connection arranged?

The answer is B. In the delta connection, all three phases are connected in series to form a closed loop. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.8

Report a problem
Objective 1.3Q780

Line voltage to a Y-Y transformer bank is 173 volts. Using √3 = 1.73, what is the voltage across each primary winding?

The answer is B. In a wye primary, winding voltage is line voltage divided by √3: 173 / 1.73 = 100 volts. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.8

Report a problem
Objective 1.3Q781

A ∆-∆ bank has a turns ratio of 5:1 and a primary line current of 10 amps. What is the secondary line current?

The answer is C. For ∆-∆, secondary line current is aI = 5 x 10 = 50 amps. In the handbook's example, a 4:1 ratio and 20 amps give 80 amps. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.11

Types of transformers

Objectives 1.4

"Distribution transformers are generally used in electrical power distribution and transmission systems." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.17

This class has the highest power ratings and the highest continuous voltage rating. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.17

"Power transformers are used in electronic circuits and come in many different types and applications." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.17
"Control transformers are generally used in electronic circuits that require constant voltage or constant current with a low power or volt-amp rating." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.18
"The auto transformer is generally used in low power applications where a variable voltage is required." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.18

The auto transformer consists of only one winding. Tapping it at certain points gives different voltages. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.18

"Isolation transformers are normally low power transformers used to isolate noise from or to ground electronic circuits." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.18

A transformer cannot pass DC voltage from primary to secondary, so DC such as noise is isolated. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.18

"The instrument potential transformer (PT) steps down voltage of a circuit to a low value that can be effectively and safely used for operation of instruments" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.19

The instrument current transformer (CT) steps down the current of a circuit to a lower value and is used in the same types of equipment. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.19

"A current transformer should always be short-circuited when not connected to an external load." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.19

Otherwise the magnetizing current builds up a large flux, inducing an excessively high voltage in the secondary under no load. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.19

Worked examples

Objective 1.1

Secondary voltage

A transformer has a primary voltage of 240 volts, 250 turns in the primary, and 50 turns in the secondary. Find the secondary voltage.

  1. VP / VS = NP / NS.
  2. VS = 240 x 50 / 250.

Answer: 48 volts

DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.6

Objective 1.3

Primary winding voltage

Line voltage is 440 V to a three-phase transformer bank. Find the voltage across each primary winding.

  1. ∆-∆ and ∆-Y: primary voltage = V = 440 volts.
  2. Y-Y and Y-∆: primary voltage = V / √3.

Answer: 440 volts for a delta primary and 254.3 volts for a wye primary

DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.10

Objective 1.3

Secondary line current

Primary line current is 20 amps and the turns ratio is 4:1. Find the secondary line current for Y-∆.

  1. Secondary line current = √3 aI.
  2. (1.73)(4)(20).

Answer: 138.4 amps

DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-13 p.11

Synchro equipment

Objectives 1.1

Position indicating instrumentation gives remote indication of control rod position and of the open or shut condition of important valves. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.1

"This remote indication is necessary for the monitoring of vital components located within inaccessible or remote areas." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.1

Synchros are self-synchronizing motors that electrically govern or follow the position of a mechanical indicator or device. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.1

"An electrical synchro has two distinct advantages over mechanical indicators: (1) greater accuracy, and (2) simpler routing of remote indication." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.1
"The basic types are: transmitters, differential transmitters, receivers, differential receivers, and control transformers." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.1
"The transmitter, or synchro generator, consists of a rotor with a single winding and a stator with three windings placed 120 degrees apart." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.1

The rotor induces a voltage in each stator winding based on its angular position. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.1

"The synchro receiver uses the voltage generated by each of the synchro generator windings to position the receiver rotor." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.2
"it incorporates a damping device to prevent hunting." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.2
"Hunting refers to the overshoot and undershoot that occur as the receiving device tries to match the sending device." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.2
"Control transformer synchros are used when only a voltage indication of angular position is desired." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.3

Limit and reed switches

Objectives 1.2

A limit switch is a mechanical device. An extension on a valve shaft mechanically trips it as the valve moves. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.5

"The limit switch gives ON/OFF output that corresponds to valve position." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.5

Normally limit switches provide full open or full shut indications. Many are the push-button variety, with spring pressure opening the switch. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.5

"Limit switch failures are normally mechanical in nature." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.6
"In this case, local position indication should be used to verify equipment position." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.6
"Reed switches, illustrated in Figure 4, are more reliable than limit switches, due to their simplified construction." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.6

Reed switches are flexible ferrous strips placed near the travel of the extension, and the extension is a permanent magnet. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.6

"As the magnet approaches the reed switch, the switch shuts. When the magnet moves away, the reed switch opens." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.6
"By using a large number of magnetic reed switches, incremental position can be measured." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.6

Failures are normally limited to a reed switch stuck open or stuck shut. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.7

"If a reed switch is stuck shut, the open (closed) indication will be continuously illuminated." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.7

If a reed switch is stuck open, the indication for that switch stays extinguished regardless of position. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.7

In a reed switch position indicator, what happens as the magnet approaches the switch?

Potentiometers and LVDTs

Objectives 1.3

A potentiometer indicator gives accurate position throughout the travel. The extension is physically attached to a variable resistor, so its movement changes the circuit's current. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.8

"The amount of current is proportional to the valve position." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.8
"An electrical short or open will cause the indication to fail at one extreme or the other." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.8
"If an increase or decrease in the potentiometer resistance occurs, erratic indicated valve position occurs." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.8
"Unlike the potentiometer position indicator, no physical connection to the extension is required." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.9

The extension acts as the movable core of a transformer. Moving it varies the inductance and output voltage in proportion to position. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.9

"A metal extension on the valve stem changes the coupling between the secondary coils." Word: Position Indicators.docx, slide 1.7 Linear Variable Differential Transformer (LVDT) valve position detector

LVDT failures are limited to rare electrical faults. An open primary winding fails the indication to a value equal to zero differential voltage, normally mid-stroke. A failed secondary winding indicates full open or full closed. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.9

Objective 1.3Q282

An open primary winding in an LVDT valve position detector makes the indication fail to:

The answer is B. An open primary fails the indication to the zero-differential value, normally mid-stroke; a failed secondary winding drives it to full open or full closed. DOE-1013-V1 (DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92), Variable Output Devices, IC-05 p. 9

Objective 1.3Q393

What does an LVDT use as the movable core of a transformer?

The answer is A. An LVDT uses the extension shaft or control rod as a movable core of a transformer, and the output voltage varies with position. DOE-1013-V1 (DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92), Variable Output Devices, IC-05 p. 10

Objective 1.3Q1008

What does an electrical short or open do to a potentiometer valve position indicator?

Objective 1.3Q1009

How does an LVDT differ from a potentiometer position indicator?

The answer is B. Unlike the potentiometer, no physical connection to the extension is required. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.9

Coil stack rod position

From the lesson plan

A coil stack is a stack of wire coils connected to an alternating current source. Word: Position Indicators.docx, slide 1.5 Coil stack control rod position

Without a core, each coil has low inductance, so circuit resistance is low and current is high. Word: Position Indicators.docx, slide 1.5 Coil stack control rod position

"As the control rod is raised, the drive mechanism becomes a core for the coils and the value of inductance goes up." Word: Position Indicators.docx, slide 1.5 Coil stack control rod position

With the drive mechanism in the middle of a coil, its inductive reactance is higher and Word: Position Indicators.docx, slide 1.5 Coil stack control rod position

"the ammeter would read a lower current." Word: Position Indicators.docx, slide 1.5 Coil stack control rod position

Position circuits and environmental concerns

Objectives 1.4, 1.5

"Detection devices provide a method to determine the position of a valve or control rod." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.13
"The indicating and control circuitry provides for remote indication of valve or rod position and/or various control functions." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.13

Reed and limit switches give open and closed indications and control functions. Potentiometer and LVDT indicators give accurate position throughout their travel. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.11

"Control functions are usually in the form of interlocks." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.11

A pump's isolation valves may be interlocked with it, to prevent starting with the valves shut or to stop the pump if a valve shuts. DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.11

"two valves may be interlocked to prevent both of the valves from being opened at the same time." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.11
"Control rod interlocks are normally used to prevent outward motion of certain rods unless certain conditions are met." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.11
"The accuracy and reliability of position indication instrumentation can be affected by ambient temperature and humidity." DOE-HDBK-1013/1-92 IC-05 p.13

Knowledge check: Position circuits and environmental concerns

Objective 1.5Q281

What should you expect from position detectors in a high temperature environment?

The answer is D. High temperature relaxes spring force, weakens permanent magnets and raises electrical resistance, so accuracy falls and failure potential rises. Approved lesson plan: Position Indicators (Position Indicators.docx), slide 1.9 How changes in the enviroment effect position detectors

What form do position control functions usually take?

Objective 1.5Q1012

Which two environmental concerns can affect position indication equipment?

Stuck open reed switch

  1. The output falls to zero differential voltage

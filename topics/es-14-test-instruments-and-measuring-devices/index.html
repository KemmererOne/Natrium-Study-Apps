Meter movements

Objectives 1.1

"The D’Arsonval movement is a DC moving coil-type movement in which an electromagnetic core is suspended between the poles of a permanent magnet." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.1

The measured current makes a magnetic field that opposes the permanent magnet and rotates the core. Springs restrain it, so the needle moves in proportion to the current. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.1

"the D’Arsonval movement is a DC device and can only measure DC current or AC current rectified" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.2

The electrodynamometer movement has the same basic principle, except that the permanent magnet is replaced by fixed coils. The fixed and moving coils are in series, so the same current flows through each. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.2

If the current is reversed, the field polarity and the polarity of the moving coil reverse, and the force continues in the same direction. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.2

"it can be used in both AC and DC systems to measure current." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.2
"its most important use is in the wattmeter" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.2
"The moving iron vane meter operates on the principle of magnetic repulsion between like poles." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.3

The current through a field coil magnetizes a fixed vane and a moving vane with the same polarity, so the moving vane pulls away and moves the pointer. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.3

Voltmeters and ammeters

Objectives 1.2, 1.3

A voltmeter measures voltage. A simple DC voltmeter has a resistor, called a multiplier, in series with the ammeter meter movement. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.5

"Voltmeters are connected in parallel with the load (RL) being measured." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.5

A connected voltmeter draws current, and the voltage drop across the meter's resistance is subtracted from the voltage being measured. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.6

"This reduction in voltage is known as the loading effect" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.6
"Normally, manufacturers counter this effect by constructing the voltmeter’s movement of an extremely high resistance to limit the current flow through the voltmeter." Word: Electrical Test Equipment.docx, slide 1.6 Voltmeter

The ammeter measures electric current. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.10

"In order to measure current, the ammeter must be placed in series with the circuit to be tested" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.10

A series ammeter increases circuit resistance by the internal resistance of the meter. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.10

"The reason for shunting an ammeter is to extend the range of the ammeter and, thereby, measure currents higher than the original full scale value." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.12

Ohm meters and wattmeters

Objectives 1.2, 1.3

The ohm meter is an instrument used to determine resistance. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.15

"Ohm meters are connected to a component which is removed from the circuit" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.15

If the component remains in the circuit and a parallel path exists, DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.15

"the current will flow in the path of least resistance and give an erroneous reading." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.15

The ohm meter is zeroed by shorting its terminals and adjusting Ro to give full-scale deflection. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.15

"The wattmeter is an instrument which measures DC power or true AC power." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.20

The fixed coils are in series with the load and indicate current. The movable coil and its multiplier are in parallel with the load and indicate voltage. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.20

"Normally, only two wattmeters are used in making 3φ power measurements" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.21

In balanced three-phase systems, total power is calculated by adding the A and B phase powers. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.21

Other measuring devices

Objectives 1.2, 1.3

The ampere-hour meter registers ampere-hours. It is a direct current meter that registers in either direction, depending on the direction of current flow. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.23

"Similar to an ammeter, the ampere-hour meter is connected in series." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.23

A power factor meter is a type of electrodynamometer movement made with two movable coils set at right angles. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.24

"Measures power factor between phases in a 3-phase circuit" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.27

It is connected in series with one phase. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.27

"The ground detector is an instrument which is used to detect conductor insulation resistance to ground." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.24
"Ground detectors measure insulation resistance to ground in ohms." Word: Electrical Test Equipment.docx, slide 1.12 Ground Detectors

In the lamp method, three lamps are connected through transformers. If the lamps are equally bright, no ground exists. If one lamp is dark and the other two are brighter, the phase with the darkened lamp is grounded. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.25

A ground detector is connected out of circuit to ground. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.27

"A synchroscope indicates when two AC generators are in the correct phase relation for connecting in parallel and shows whether the incoming generator is running faster or slower than the on-line generator." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.26
"if incoming frequency is higher than running frequency, it will rotate in the clockwise direction" DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.26

When it indicates 0° phase difference, the pointer is at the 12 o'clock position and the two AC generators are in phase. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.26

It is connected by a two-phase stator at right angles. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.27

Test equipment

Objectives 1.3, 1.4

"The multimeter is a portable single instrument capable of measuring various electrical values including voltage, resistance, and current." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.28

The volt-ohm-milliammeter (VOM) is the most commonly used multimeter. Range switches are usually provided for scale selection. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.28

"The megger is a portable instrument used to measure insulation resistance." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.28

It consists of a hand-driven DC generator and a direct reading ohm meter. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.28

With the test terminals open, the pointer moves to infinite resistance. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.28

With the terminals shorted, it moves to zero resistance. DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.29

"Meggers are connected out of circuit." DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.30

Worked examples

Objective 1.3

Voltmeter multiplier

A 2 mA meter movement with internal resistance of 25 ohms is to measure a full scale voltage of 100 volts. Find the series resistance.

  1. Rs = (V / Im) - Rm.
  2. Since Rm is negligibly low, Rs = 100 V / 2 mA.

Answer: 50 kΩ

DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.6

Objective 1.3

Ohm meter deflection

An ohm meter has Ro = 30 Ω and a full scale current of 300 µA. A 5 Ω resistor is connected. With a deflection factor of 0.86, find the current.

  1. I = D Im.
  2. I = (0.86)(300 x 10⁻⁶).

Answer: 258 µA

DOE-HDBK-1011/4-92 ES-14 p.18

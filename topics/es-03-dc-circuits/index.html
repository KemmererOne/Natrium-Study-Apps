Inductors

Objectives 1.1, 1.2

"An inductor is a circuit element that will store electrical energy in the form of a magnetic field." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.1

An inductor is usually a coil of wire wrapped around a core of permeable material. The magnetic field is generated when current flows through the wire. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.1

A voltage is induced in a conductor when it moves through a magnetic field, or when the field moves past it. This is Faraday's Law. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.2

"the induced EMF opposes the EMF that caused it." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.2

An induced EMF is sometimes called counter EMF, or CEMF. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.2

If the switch in a circuit with a coil is opened, the field around the inductor collapses, and the collapsing field produces a voltage in the coil. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.2

"the current caused by the induced EMF tends to maintain the same current that existed in the circuit before the switch was opened." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.2
"It is commonly said that an inductor tends to oppose a change in current flow." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.2
"Inductance is a measure of an inductor’s ability to induce CEMF." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.3

Inductance is measured in henries. An inductor has an inductance of one henry if one amp per second change in current produces one volt of CEMF. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.3

CEMF = -L (ΔI / Δt) DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.3

Combining inductors and the time constant

Objectives 1.3, 1.4

"Inductors in series are combined like resistors in series." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.8

Leq = L1 + L2 + ... Ln DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.3

"Inductors in parallel are combined like resistors in parallel." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.8

Leq = (L1 x L2) / (L1 + L2), for two inductors only DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.4

In circuits with elements that store energy, it is common for current and voltage to exhibit exponential increase and decay. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.4

"The time constant for an inductor is defined as the time required for the current either to increase to 63.2 percent of its maximum value or to decrease by 63.2 percent of its maximum value" DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.5

The time constant is directly proportional to the inductance and inversely proportional to the resistance. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.5

TL = L / R DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.5

"After five time constants, circuit parameters normally reach their final value." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.6

Objective 1.3Q657

What is the equivalent inductance of a 2 henry and a 3 henry inductor in series?

The answer is B. Inductors in series are combined like resistors in series: 2 + 3 = 5 henries. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.8

Objective 1.3Q658

What is the equivalent inductance of a 6 henry and a 3 henry inductor in parallel?

The answer is A. Inductors in parallel are combined like resistors in parallel: (6 x 3) / (6 + 3) = 2 henries. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.8

Objective 1.4Q659

What is the time constant of a circuit with 10 henries and 5 ohms?

The answer is B. TL = L/R = 10/5 = 2 seconds. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.5

Capacitors

Objectives 1.5, 1.6, 1.7

"Electrical devices that are constructed of two metal plates separated by an insulating material, called a dielectric, are known as capacitors" DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.9

When a battery is connected across the plates, charges move until the difference in charge between the plates is equal to the voltage of the battery. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.10

"Capacitors store energy as an electric field between the two plates." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.10

Very few charges can cross between the plates, so the capacitor stays charged even if the battery is removed. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.10

"The attraction of charges in the opposite plates of a capacitor opposes a change in voltage across the capacitor." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.18

Placing a conductor across the plates neutralizes the charges, and the capacitor is discharged. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.10

"A farad is the capacitance that will store one coulomb of charge when one volt is applied across the plates of the capacitor." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.11
"The capacitance of a capacitor depends on three things." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.11
  • Area of conductor plates
  • Separation between the plates
  • Dielectric constant of insulation material

DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.11

Combining capacitors and the time constant

Objectives 1.8, 1.9

"Capacitors in series are combined like resistors in parallel." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.13

1/CT = 1/C1 + 1/C2 + ... DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.13

CT = (C1 x C2) / (C1 + C2), for two capacitors only DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.14

"Capacitors in parallel are combined like resistors in series." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.14

CT = C1 + C2 + ... DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.14

Total voltage across capacitors in series is the sum of their working voltages. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.15

The working voltage of capacitors in parallel is only as high as the lowest individual working voltage. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.16

Resistance in a circuit causes a delay in the time for charging a capacitor. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.16

TC = RC DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.16

"The capacitive time constant is the time required for the capacitor to charge to 63.2 percent of its fully charged voltage." DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.16

In each following time constant, the capacitor charges an additional 63.2 percent of the remaining voltage. It is considered fully charged after five time constants. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.16

Worked examples

Objective 1.2

CEMF of an inductor

The current through a 4 henry inductor drops from 6 amps to 2 amps in 2 seconds. What is the CEMF?

  1. CEMF = -L (ΔI / Δt).
  2. ΔI / Δt = (2 - 6) / 2 = -2 amps per second.
  3. CEMF = -(4)(-2).

Answer: 8 volts

DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.3

Objective 1.8

Capacitors in parallel

Find the total capacitance of 15 µF-50 V, 10 µF-100 V, and 3 µF-150 V capacitors in parallel. What is the working voltage?

  1. CT = 15 + 10 + 3.
  2. The working voltage is only as high as the lowest individual working voltage.

Answer: 28 µF, with a working voltage of 50 volts

DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.16

Objective 1.8

Capacitors in series

Find the working voltage of two capacitors in series, each rated 120 V.

  1. Total voltage across capacitors in series is the sum of the individual working voltages.
  2. Working voltage = 120 V + 120 V.

Answer: 240 volts

DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.15

Objective 1.9

Capacitive time constant

Find the time constant of a 100 µF capacitor in series with a 100 Ω resistor.

  1. TC = RC.
  2. TC = (100 Ω)(100 µF).

Answer: 0.01 seconds

DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.18

Summary

  • When an inductor has DC current flowing through it, it stores energy in the form of a magnetic field. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.8
  • An inductor opposes a change in current flow by the CEMF induced when the field collapses or expands. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.8
  • Inductors combine like resistors, in series and in parallel. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.8
  • A capacitor is two conductors separated by a dielectric, and it stores energy in an electric field. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.18
  • Capacitors in series combine like resistors in parallel, and capacitors in parallel combine like resistors in series. DOE-HDBK-1011/2-92 ES-03 p.18

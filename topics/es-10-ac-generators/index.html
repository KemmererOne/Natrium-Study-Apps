AC generator components

Objectives 1.1

"The field in an AC generator consists of coils of conductors within the generator that receive a voltage from a source (called excitation) and produce a magnetic flux." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.1

The magnetic flux in the field cuts the armature to produce a voltage. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.1

"The armature is the part of an AC generator in which voltage is produced." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.1

The armature consists of many coils of wire large enough to carry the full-load current. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.1

"The prime mover is the component that is used to drive the AC generator." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.1
"The rotor of an AC generator is the rotating component of the generator" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.2

The rotor is driven by the prime mover. It may be the armature or the field, depending on the type of generator. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.2

"The stator of an AC generator is the part that is stationary" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.2

Like the rotor, the stator may be the armature or the field. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.2

"Slip rings are electrical connections that are used to transfer power to and from the rotor of an AC generator" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.3

Slip rings allow the output current and voltage to oscillate through positive and negative values, in the shape of a sine wave. A DC generator uses a commutator instead. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.3

Frequency, losses, and efficiency

Objectives 1.2, 1.3, 1.4

"The magnitude of AC voltage generated by an AC generator is dependent on the field strength and speed of the rotor." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.5

Most generators are operated at a constant speed, so the generated voltage depends on field excitation. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.5

"The frequency of the generated voltage is dependent on the number of field poles and the speed at which the generator is operated" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.6

f = NP / 120 DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.6

f is frequency in Hz, P is the total number of poles, and N is rotor speed in rpm. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.6

There are three losses in an AC generator: DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.8

  • Internal voltage drops, due to the internal resistance and impedance of the armature
  • Hysteresis losses
  • Mechanical losses

DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.8

"The combination of these make up what is known as the internal resistance, which causes a loss in an AC generator." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.6

Hysteresis losses occur when iron cores are subject to effects from a magnetic field. Rotating magnetic domains cause friction and heat. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.7

Mechanical losses can be caused by bearing friction, brush friction, and air friction, called windage. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.7

"Efficiency of an AC generator is the ratio of the useful power output to the total power input." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.7

Efficiency = (Output / Input) x 100 DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.7

Input and output power must be in the same units. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.7

Knowledge check: Frequency, losses, and efficiency

Objective 1.2Q430

How is the frequency of an AC generator calculated?

The answer is B. Frequency is the number of poles multiplied by the speed of the generator, divided by a factor of 120. DOE-1011-V3 (DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92), AC Generator Theory, ES-10 p. 8

Report a problem
Objective 1.2Q749

What is the output frequency of a 2 pole generator running at 3000 rpm?

The answer is B. f = NP / 120 = (3000)(2) / 120 = 50 Hz. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.6

Report a problem
Objective 1.3Q750

Which of these is NOT one of the three losses in an AC generator?

The answer is D. The three losses are internal voltage drops, hysteresis losses, and mechanical losses. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.6

Report a problem
Objective 1.4Q751

A prime mover delivers 5000 watts, and the generator output is 4000 watts. What is the efficiency?

The answer is C. Efficiency = (Output / Input) x 100 = (4000 / 5000) x 100 = 80 percent. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.7

Ratings

Objectives 1.5

Typical nameplate data includes speed, number of poles, frequency, number of phases, maximum supply voltage, capacity in KVA and kW at a specified power factor, armature and field current per phase, and maximum temperature rise. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.9

"Power (kW) ratings of an AC generator are based on the ability of the prime mover to overcome generator losses and the ability of the machine to dissipate the internally generated heat." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.9

The current rating of an AC generator is based on the insulation rating of the machine. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.14

Paralleling AC generators

Objectives 1.6

Two or more generators are normally operated in parallel to increase the available power. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

"Three conditions must be met prior to paralleling (or synchronizing) AC" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10
  • Their terminal voltages must be equal.
  • Their frequencies must be equal.
  • Their output voltages must be in phase.

DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

"If the voltages of the two AC generators are not equal, one of the AC generators could be picked up as a reactive load to the other AC generator." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

This causes high currents between the two machines, possibly causing generator or distribution system damage. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

"A mismatch in frequencies of the two AC generators will cause the generator with the lower frequency to be picked up as a load on the other generator" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

This condition is referred to as motoring, and it can cause an overload in the generators and the distribution system. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

A mismatch in the phases will cause large opposing voltages to be developed. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

"The worst case mismatch would be 180° out of phase, resulting in an opposing voltage between the two generators of twice the output voltage." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

Voltages are indicated by voltmeters, and frequency matching uses output frequency meters. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

"Phase matching is accomplished through the use of a synchroscope" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

Types of AC generators

Objectives 1.7

"Small AC generators usually have a stationary field and a rotating armature" DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

One disadvantage is that the slip-ring and brush assembly is in series with the load circuits and, because of worn or dirty components, may interrupt the flow of current. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.10

If DC field excitation is connected to the rotor, the stationary coils will have AC induced into them. This is a rotating field, stationary armature AC generator. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.11

The rotating field, stationary DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.11

"armature type AC generator is used when large power generation is involved." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.11

Its advantages: a load can be connected to the armature without moving contacts in the circuit, it is much easier to insulate stator fields than rotating fields, and much higher voltages and currents can be generated. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.11

Three-phase generators and connections

Objectives 1.8

A three-phase generator has three equally-spaced windings and three output voltages, all 120° out of phase with one another. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.11

In a delta connection, the windings are connected in series. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.12

The voltage between any two phases, called line voltage, is the same as the voltage generated in any one phase. The current in any line is √3 times the phase current. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.12

"You can see that a delta-connected generator provides an increase in current, but no increase in voltage." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.12
"An advantage of the delta-connected AC generator is that if one phase becomes damaged or open, the remaining two phases can still deliver three-phase power." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.12

With one phase out, the capacity of the generator is reduced to 57.7% of what it was. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.12

In a wye connection, one of the leads of each winding is connected, and the remaining three leads are connected to an external load. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.13

Voltage between any two lines is 1.73 times any one phase voltage, while line currents are equal to phase currents. DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.13

"An advantage of a wye-connected AC generator is that each phase only has to carry 57.7% of line voltage and, therefore, can be used for high voltage generation." DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.13

Objective 1.4

Generator efficiency

A 5 hp motor is the prime mover of a generator with a load demand of 2 kW. What is the efficiency?

  1. Input power = 5 hp x 746 W/hp = 3730 W.
  2. Output power = 2 kW = 2000 W.
  3. Efficiency = (2000 / 3730) x 100.

Answer: 54%

DOE-HDBK-1011/3-92 ES-10 p.8

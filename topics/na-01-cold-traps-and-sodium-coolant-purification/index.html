Why sodium must be purified

Objectives 1

"liquid sodium is subject to the formation of impurities due to its high chemical reactivity" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.2 Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification
"The impurities are mainly in the form of hydrides, oxides, metallic compounds, metallic and carbon particles" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.2 Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification

They come from these sources: Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.2 Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification

"originate primarily from corrosion, moisture from system component surfaces, and air intrusion into the system" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.2 Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification
"these impurities would collect in the form of crystallized sodium hydride (NaH) or sodium oxide (Na2O) at the cold points of the sodium loops" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.2 Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification

Left alone, this may clog piping sections or fuel channels, or damage pumps and other components. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.2 Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification

"Pure liquid sodium is not very corrosive." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.4 Importance of purifying sodium coolant

Sodium can react with impurities in the molten metal to form oxides, which can corrode structural materials. Corrosion products can dissolve, transfer and deposit on reactor structures and core components. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.4 Importance of purifying sodium coolant

"These become activated, and can reduce heat transfer in the reactor core." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.4 Importance of purifying sodium coolant
"the formation of sodium hydroxide can lead to rapid and aggressive caustic stress corrosion cracking" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.4 Importance of purifying sodium coolant
"Cold traps are the main devices for the purification of sodium in fast reactor applications." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.2 Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification

Safety minute: BN-600

From the cited sources

The BN-600 is a sodium-cooled fast breeder reactor at the Beloyarsk Nuclear Power Station in Russia. On 21 January 1987, with the reactor at rated power, changes were noted for 7 minutes in sodium level, core reactivity and sodium pump power. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.3 Safety Minute

"the incident was attributed to the drop of a chunk of impurities from the reactor roof, formed from impurities in the cover gas" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.3 Safety Minute

The block of impurities disturbed core hydraulics and neutronics. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.3 Safety Minute

"This event later resulted in several clad failures and a reactor shutdown." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.3 Safety Minute

Since then, leaks into the core cover gas have been minimized, and more advanced purification is done. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.3 Safety Minute

What must be removed

Objectives 1

"Impurities such as hydrogen, oxygen, tritium, cesium and others must be removed." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.8 Sodium Coolant Impurities

Other impurities include metals such as iron and chromium, some of their active isotopes activated by neutron flux, carbonated particles and calcium oxide. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.8 Sodium Coolant Impurities

"Cold Traps remove oxygen, hydrogen, and carbon impurities from the primary and intermediate sodium coolant loops." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.7 Component Purpose

These impurities precipitate out of the coolant at optimum temperatures and collect in the trap device. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.7 Component Purpose

"Reticulated Vitreous Carbon Filters remove Cesium from the primary system." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.7 Component Purpose

They use chemical adsorption to strip the cesium from the sodium coolant. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.7 Component Purpose

Objective 1Q124

What is the main purpose of a cold trap in an SFR?

The answer is B. A cold trap cools sodium below the saturation temperature of impurities so they crystallize. Its main purpose is oxygen removal, and it also removes hydrogen (tritium), carbon and some corrosion products. How a cold trap works

Objectives 2

"A cold trap operates by cooling a small fraction of the flow in the main piping system to temperatures just above the freezing point of sodium." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.10 Cold Trap
"the solubility of oxides, hydroxides, or other impurities is drastically reduced" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.10 Cold Trap

Dirty sodium that enters the trap becomes supersaturated with impurities as it cools. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.10 Cold Trap

"This allows for nucleation and subsequent crystal growth." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.10 Cold Trap

The impurities precipitate out of solution and stay in the trap: Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.10 Cold Trap

"adhere to the stainless-steel mesh packing and walls within the volume of the cold trap" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.10 Cold Trap

The clean, cool sodium can then reenter the main loop as the cleaning continues. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.10 Cold Trap

To cool the sodium, the cold trap loop relies on an economizer and a blower that pushes cooling fluid over the trap's heat transfer fins. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.10 Cold Trap

"The economizer acts as a regenerative heat exchanger and pre-heats the clean sodium re-entering the system." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.10 Cold Trap
"impurity concentration levels under five parts per million (ppm) can be achieved" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.10 Cold Trap

Sodium temperature in the trap

Objectives 4

Cold traps cool liquid sodium to just above the sodium freezing point. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.12 Cold traps

"Typically, this temperature range falls between 230°F and 300°F." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.12 Cold traps
"If sodium temperature is too high, particulates may not effectively precipitate out of the coolant" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.12 Cold traps

In that case the impurity removal capability of the cold trap is reduced. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.12 Cold traps

"If sodium temperatures are too low, there is a risk of sodium freezing and solidification." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.12 Cold traps

Freezing could block areas of the system, the cold trap or the plugging temperature circuit. Recovery from frozen sodium can be difficult and costly. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.12 Cold traps

Cooling can use a heat exchanger or blower: Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.12 Cold traps

"forced air, nitrogen or argon serve as the cooling fluid" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.12 Cold traps

Impurities and plugging temperature

Objectives 3, 6

As sodium approaches its freezing point, impurities precipitate out of solution and begin to crystallize. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.15 Plugging Temperature Indicator

"As impurities build up in the coolant, the temperature at which crystallization occurs rises." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.15 Plugging Temperature Indicator
"A plugging temperature indicator measures flow past an orifice." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.15 Plugging Temperature Indicator

When the sodium at the orifice reaches the impurity saturation temperature, impurities such as oxygen and hydrogen begin to precipitate and deposit on the orifice surface, changing the flow rate. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.15 Plugging Temperature Indicator

The temperature measured at the moment the flow begins to decrease is the plugging temperature. Monitoring its changes helps determine the level of impurities in the coolant. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.15 Plugging Temperature Indicator

"The more the impurities the higher the temperature a decrease in flow rate will be detected." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.17 Sodium Purity Monitoring

Removing radionuclides: RVC filters

Objectives 8

"the removal of radionuclides in the sodium coolant is necessary to maintain coolant purity and reduce radiation levels in the plant" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.26 Reticulated Vitreous Carbon (RVC) Filters

Cesium traps, typically containing reticulated vitreous carbon (RVC) filters, are used to meet this goal. Reticulated means net-like, and vitreous means like glass in appearance or qualities. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.26 Reticulated Vitreous Carbon (RVC) Filters

"Adsorption is a process by which chemical bonds are formed between the adsorbed material and the surface of the filter media." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.26 Reticulated Vitreous Carbon (RVC) Filters

This forms a stronger attraction than physical adsorption. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.26 Reticulated Vitreous Carbon (RVC) Filters

"Use of RVC filters has been shown to reduce Cs-137 activity by a factor of 10." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.26 Reticulated Vitreous Carbon (RVC) Filters
"RVC is a rigid, non-graphitizable carbon foam with a honeycomb type structure, with very low density." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.28 Reticulated Vitreous Carbon
  • High surface area
  • Low resistance to fluid flow
  • Low density and thermal expansion
  • High corrosion resistance
  • Rigid structure

Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.28 Reticulated Vitreous Carbon

A trap accumulator RVC unit is similar in design and construction to a cold trap, but it contains RVC foam. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.29 RVC Vessel Types

"Instead of using the property of physical precipitation of impurities, the chemical process of adsorption drives the operation of an RVC unit." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.29 RVC Vessel Types

Its operating temperature is also higher than a cold trap's. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.29 RVC Vessel Types

"Optimal operating conditions for RVC filtration are a trapping temperature between ~350°F and 550°F." Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.28 Reticulated Vitreous Carbon

In this range: Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.28 Reticulated Vitreous Carbon

"the chemical adsorption process is very efficient, as high temperatures contribute to the process" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.28 Reticulated Vitreous Carbon

Another design is the MAVR type, a Russian acronym for "Compact Adsorber for removal of Radioactivity." It uses a low-ash granular graphite filter medium. This filter: Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.29 RVC Vessel Types

"is inserted into the reactor core, as opposed to a separate coolant purification loop" Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.29 RVC Vessel Types

Objective 4

Cold trap sodium running too hot or too cold

A cold trap normally runs in the typical 230°F to 300°F range. What is the risk if its sodium runs above that range, and what is the risk if it is cooled too far below it?

  1. Too high: particulates may not effectively precipitate, so impurity removal is reduced.
  2. Too low: the sodium may freeze and solidify.
  3. Frozen sodium could block the system, the trap or the plugging temperature circuit, and recovery can be difficult and costly.

Answer: Too hot reduces impurity removal. Too cold risks freezing and blockage.

Summary

  • Impurities form in sodium from corrosion, moisture and air intrusion, and would collect as crystals at cold points. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.2 Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification
  • Cold traps are the main sodium purification devices. They cool a small fraction of the flow to just above the sodium freezing point so impurities precipitate on the mesh. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.10 Cold Trap
  • Typical cold trap temperature is 230°F to 300°F. Too hot reduces removal, and too cold risks freezing. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.12 Cold traps
  • As impurities build up, the temperature at which crystallization occurs rises. A plugging temperature indicator uses this. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.15 Plugging Temperature Indicator
  • Temperature and flow are controlled to avoid liquid oxygen, sodium oxide plugging and sodium freezing. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.20 Cautions
  • A loaded cold trap is regenerated ex-situ or in-situ. Word: Cold Traps and Sodium Coolant Purification.docx, slide 1.21 Regeneration

Chapter 12 describes how the plant goes from construction to full power. Know the four stages, who does each, and what happens in each. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Ch. 12, pp. 12.1-1 to 12.6-1

1 State the objectives of the Post-Construction Inspection, Testing, and Analysis Program.

Taught in: Program objectives

2 Identify the four stages and who performs each.

Taught in: Stages and roles

3 Describe construction, component, and system testing.

Taught in: Construction, component, and system testing

4 Describe initial fuel load, low power, and power ascension testing.

Taught in: Initial startup testing

Program objectives

Objectives 1

The primary objective is to show the plant was built as designed and that safety-significant SSCs perform consistent with the design. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.1, p. 12.1-1

It also shows that fuel load, criticality, and power ascension are done in a controlled and safe manner. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.1, p. 12.1-1

It gives assurance that operators know the procedures and are prepared to operate safely. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.1, p. 12.1-1

A graded approach is used. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.1, p. 12.1-2

Construction, component, and system testing

Objectives 3

  • Cable separation, continuity, megger, and high potential testing
  • Non-destructive testing of welds
  • Hydrostatic testing of piping
  • Hanger and pipe support inspections
  • Pump and valve alignments
  • Cleaning and flushing

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.3, p. 12.3-1

Preoperational testing covers Stages 2 and 3. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.4, p. 12.4-1

  • System flushing and cleanliness verification
  • Electrical equipment testing
  • Loop checks and calibrations
  • Control system checks
  • Instrument calibrations
  • Individual component testing

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.4.1, p. 12.4-1

Component testing shows a component is ready to move from a static to a dynamic state. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.4.1, p. 12.4-1

Stage 3 covers individual system testing, then integrated system testing. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.4, p. 12.4-1

Stage 3 starts the test review group, which manages procedure changes and reviews results. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.4.2, p. 12.4-1

Initial startup testing

Objectives 4

Details of Phase 2 testing will be given at the OL stage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.5, p. 12.5-1

Fuel load starts after preoperational testing, handover to operations, and issuance of the operating license and operator licenses. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.5.1, p. 12.5-1

Post-core hot function testing comes after fuel load and before criticality. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.5.1, p. 12.5-1

Low-power physics tests verify selected core design parameters. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.5.2, p. 12.5-1

  • Reactivity control system rod worth
  • Neutron monitoring operation and calibration
  • Neutron flux distribution
  • Neutron and gamma radiation surveys
  • Operability of alarms and low power protective features
  • Reactivity control system performance

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.5.2, p. 12.5-1

Power ascension shows the plant operates as designed in steady state and, as practicable, in anticipated transients. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.5.3, p. 12.5-2

Test hold points review results and confirm prerequisites before the next step. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 12.5.1, p. 12.5-1

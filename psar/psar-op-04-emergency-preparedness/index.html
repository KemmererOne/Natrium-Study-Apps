The emergency plan itself comes at the operating license stage. The PSAR sets out the performance-based approach, who is in charge, and what facilities will exist. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 11.3, pp. 11.3-1 to 11.3-9

Taught in: Government agencies

Performance-based approach

Objectives 1

The emergency preparedness plan will be provided at the OL stage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3, p. 11.3-1

It will be developed using 10 CFR 50.160 and RG 1.242. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3, p. 11.3-1

The approach is performance-based, technology-inclusive, risk-informed, and consequence-oriented. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.1, p. 11.3-1

A site-specific PRA will be used to form the plan and inform EPZ size. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.1, p. 11.3-1

Plan reviews follow 10 CFR 50.54(t), and changes follow 10 CFR 50.54(q). PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.1, p. 11.3-3

Emergency Planning Zone

Objectives 2

RG 1.242 Appendix A was used to determine EPZ size. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.3.1, p. 11.3-3

The preliminary analysis supports a PEP EPZ within the EAB, meeting 10 CFR 50.33(g)(2)(i). PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.3.1, p. 11.3-3

So there is no requirement for pre-planned off-site protective actions, but capabilities support recommendations to off-site agencies. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.6.3, p. 11.3-6

Security events are not specifically identified in the preliminary analysis. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.3.1, p. 11.3-3

Emergency Response Organization

Objectives 3

The Shift Manager functions as the Emergency Director (ED) until duties transfer to an ERF ED. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.4.2, p. 11.3-4

The on-shift health physics person supports the ED until duties transfer to an ERF. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.4.2, p. 11.3-4

The ED assumes command and control after an emergency declaration. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.4.2, p. 11.3-4

ERO staffing depends on the functions needed for postulated emergencies. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.4, p. 11.3-4

Government agencies

Objectives 4

The NRC is the primary authority for radiological events at NRC-licensed facilities. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.5.1, p. 11.3-5

For incidents affecting the public, FEMA may coordinate the federal response. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.5.1, p. 11.3-5

Notification protocols are set with the Wyoming Office of Homeland Security, Lincoln County, and Kemmerer. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.5.2, p. 11.3-5

Letters of agreement will be submitted with the plan. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.5.2, p. 11.3-5

Protective measures

Objectives 5

  • First aid
  • Moving personnel away from hazardous areas
  • Protective gear
  • Contamination control
  • Establishing restricted areas
  • Site evacuation of non-essential personnel
  • Coordination with the fire protection and security plans

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.6.1, p. 11.3-5

The most likely on-site actions are protective equipment, sheltering, or site evacuation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.6.3, p. 11.3-6

An emergency classification scheme with Emergency Action Levels will come with the plan. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.6.2, p. 11.3-6

Medical treatment or decontamination is prioritized by injury severity and contamination level. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.7, p. 11.3-6

Facilities and communications

Objectives 6

A primary ERF (on-site) and a backup ERF (off-site) will be available. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.9.1, p. 11.3-7

Their functions are alike and similar to a Technical Support Center and Operational Support Center. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.9.1, p. 11.3-7

ERF staff relieve operators of duties not directly tied to reactor control. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.9.1, p. 11.3-7

The ERF is the primary communications center during an emergency. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.9.1, p. 11.3-7

Dose projections will use real-time meteorological information. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.9.2.1, p. 11.3-7

Capabilities for 24-hour notification will be provided. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.1, p. 11.3-2

Training and performance

Objectives 7

ERO members get initial training before assignment. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.8, p. 11.3-6

Off-site personnel get site familiarization training. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.8, p. 11.3-6

Drills and exercises demonstrate the functions in 10 CFR 50.160(b)(1)(iii)(A) through (H). PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.10, p. 11.3-8

Drills, exercises, and real events are critiqued to find weaknesses. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.3.10, p. 11.3-8

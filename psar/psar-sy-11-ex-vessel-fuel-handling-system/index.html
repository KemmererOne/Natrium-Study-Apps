Purpose and path of a core assembly

Objectives 1, 2

The FHE moves new core assemblies from receipt through inspection and conditioning to the reactor vessel. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1, p. 7.3-17

It keeps an assembly in an inert environment from the NAPS to the reactor, and from the reactor until the assembly is in the PIC and lowered into the SFP. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1, p. 7.3-17

The shielded, inert transfer provides radiation shielding, radionuclide retention, and a medium for decay heat removal. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1, p. 7.3-17

ComponentMoves or does
EVHMMoves CAPs between the reactor vessel in the RXB and the EVST in the FHB PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-17
BLTCMoves assemblies between the NAPS, PIC, and EVST PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.2, p. 7.3-19
EVSTTemporary storage for new and irradiated assemblies PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.3, p. 7.3-20
NAPSPreconditions and stores new assemblies PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-32
PRCHot cell for pin removal from LDAs and LTAs PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.7, p. 7.3-22

FHE interfaces with the FHI for in-vessel handling and the FHP for fuel pool handling. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2, p. 7.3-17

Ex-Vessel Handling Machine

Objectives 3, 4

The EVHM is a rail-mounted, shielded, sealable vessel transport system. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-17

The shielded cask assembly gives shielding and pressure boundary management, keeps an argon inert environment, and keeps fuel within design temperatures. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-18

The EVHM grapple does not directly handle any reactor core assemblies. It handles the CAP. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-18

Each CAP is filled with liquid sodium and acts as the heat sink and conduction path. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-19

A passive siphon lowers the CAP sodium level as it leaves the sodium, to minimize spillage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-19

The cask is lead and B4C. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-28

Cask cooling on loss of power

Objectives 4, 8

In EVHM cooling operations, exhaust blowers pull air through the annulus between the main shielding cask and the cold wall. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-18

For heating, dampers shut and resistive heaters energize. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-18

If the EVHM loses its plant side power, the dampers fail open and natural convection keeps cooling. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-18

The EVHM passive heat removal system has a maximum heat capacity of 15.2 kW. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-19

The EVHM passive system is designed for core assemblies 7 days after shutdown. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-18

The BLTC handles assemblies only after a storage time in the EVST. It is designed for a 1.2 kW assembly. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-30

The BLTC dampers also fail open on loss of plant power. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.2, p. 7.3-20

EVHM15.2 kW PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-19
BLTC1.2 kW PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-30

Ex-Vessel Storage Tank

Objectives 3, 4, 8

The EVST is a sodium-filled tank with a rotating carousel and storage for 100 core assemblies in CAPs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.3, p. 7.3-20

The carousel holds assemblies in a subcritical array by geometry. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.3, p. 7.3-21

The striker plate protects the EVST from accidentally dropped external loads. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.3, p. 7.3-20

In a main vessel leak, the guard vessel captures sodium while main vessel level stays above the fueled portion of the assemblies. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.3, p. 7.3-21

The EVST is designed for a maximum decay heat of 216 kW and a normal decay heat of 136 kW. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-29

Passive heat removal uses inlet and outlet stacks and ducts to circulate air through the EVST vault. Natural convection carries heat to the atmosphere, the ultimate heat sink. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-29

Passive cooling keeps cladding below the maximum pin cladding temperature of 1157 F. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-30

Penetrations are above the minimum safe sodium level. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.3, p. 7.3-21

A failure of the NST cooling and temperature control systems would not stop the SR passive heat removal function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.3, p. 7.3-21

Isolation valves on the main vessel close on detection of a leak or on loss of power. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-30

With little decay heat, trace heaters on the guard vessel and the sodium processing loop keep sodium temperature in limits. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.3, p. 7.3-21

Boundaries during transfer

Objectives 2, 3

The FFV is a temporary closure that allows transfers at ports while keeping an inert containment boundary. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.5, p. 7.3-22

The FFV normal internal range is 250 to 400 F and 0.3 to 0.4 psig. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.5, p. 7.3-22

The CATT gives an inert passage between the EVHM and the reactor vessel and is installed only during Mode 4. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.6, p. 7.3-22

CATT argon holds a positive pressure, and its seals are redundant. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.6, p. 7.3-22

The CATT cavity minimum temperature is 400 F, which prevents sodium solidification. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.6, p. 7.3-22

In Modes 1, 2, and 3, the functional containment boundary is coincident with the primary coolant boundary. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-29

Each BLTC inflatable seal is designed to stay inflated for 72 hours on loss of primary argon supply. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-28

The EVHM and BLTC use rail-based seismic restraints that limit uplift or tipping. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-29

Other stations

Objectives 3, 4

The NAPS holds 8 core assemblies and prevents criticality by a subcritical geometry. It is NST. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-32

The ARIS on the FHB operating floor holds assemblies for new fuel receipt inspection. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-32

In the PRC, a sodium holding tank under the FFV receives an LDA or LTA in a CAP. Heat leaves by free convection. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.7, p. 7.3-23

The PEM is a remotely operated handling device that removes pins from the LTA. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-32

Controls and interlocks

Objectives 5, 6

Fuel handling during refueling is controlled by a series of interlocks. EVHM and BLTC controls and interlocks prevent load drops and handling accidents. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.5, p. 7.3-33

The carousel positioning system is semi-automatic. Interlocks stop repositioning when damage would result. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.5, p. 7.3-33

It verifies that all transfer port plugs are in place, or that the grapple is fully retracted or not at the port with the FFV closed. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.5, p. 7.3-33

  • Carousel is stopped
  • Position agrees with the address requested, except in MANUAL
  • Carousel position aligns a storage location with an open port
  • EVHM or BLTC is over the correct floor valve

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.5, p. 7.3-33

Operators use human-machine interface units in the FHB control room, which are part of the PMC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.5, p. 7.3-33

Hoist motors, gearboxes, and brakes are outside the hoist enclosure and can be manually moved to lower the load without breaking the containment barrier. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-29

EVST temperature sensors let operators see whether cladding limits are being approached. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.2, p. 7.3-26

Summary

  • The FHE keeps assemblies in a shielded, inert environment. On the EVHM they ride in sodium-filled CAPs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1, p. 7.3-17
  • Cask dampers fail open on loss of power, so natural convection keeps cooling. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.1.1, p. 7.3-18
  • The EVST holds 100 assemblies in a subcritical geometry and removes up to 216 kW passively. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.2.3, p. 7.3-29

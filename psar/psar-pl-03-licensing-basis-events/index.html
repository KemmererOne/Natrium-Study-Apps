The PSAR uses a risk-informed framework. This lesson explains how events are found, sorted by frequency, analyzed, and judged. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Secs. 3.1 and 3.3 to 3.9, with Sec. 4.1

Terms with a dotted underline are PSAR acronyms. Hover, tap or press Enter on one to see its meaning.

Learning objectives

1 Describe the role of the PRA in selecting licensing basis events.

Taught in: The PRA and LBE selection

2 Classify AOOs, DBEs, and BDBEs by frequency, and describe DBAs.

Taught in: Event categories

3 Explain how analysis assumptions differ between DBAs and other LBEs.

Taught in: Analysis assumptions

4 State the initial conditions and end states used in LBE analysis.

Taught in: Initial conditions and end states

5 Explain what makes an LBE risk-significant or a BDBE high-consequence.

Taught in: Risk significance

The PRA and LBE selection

Objectives 1

The PRA is the plant model that provides an integrated assessment of risk to the public. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.1, p. 3.1-1

The NEI 18-04 methodology, endorsed by RG 1.233, is implemented without deviation for LBE selection and evaluation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.4, p. 3.4-1

LBE selection includes the systematic definition, categorization, and evaluation of event sequences. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.4, p. 3.4-1

Radionuclide sources in the PRA include the RES, SPS and SCG, gaseous radwaste, the EVHM, the EVST, spent fuel processing, and the Spent Fuel Pool. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.1.1.3, p. 3.1-2

At the construction permit stage, the PRA is internal events at-power and low-power shutdown only. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.1.1.15, p. 3.1-7

Event categories

Objectives 2

BDBEs are event sequences with mean frequencies of 5x10^-7 to 1x10^-4 per plant-year. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8, p. 3.8-1

The DBE frequency ceiling used in the DID evaluation is 1x10^-2 per plant-year, and the BDBE ceiling is 1x10^-4. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1, p. 4.2-2

Tables 3.5-1, 3.5-2, and 3.5-3 summarize the AOOs, DBEs, and BDBEs. Table 3.5-4 maps DBEs to DBAs and gives each DBA's dose consequence. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.5.2, p. 3.5-1

Objective 2PL-03-K2

A sequence with a mean frequency of 1x10^-5 per plant-year is classified as what?

The answer is C. Mean frequencies of 5x10^-7 to 1x10^-4 per plant-year are classified as BDBEs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8, p. 3.8-1

Analysis assumptions

Objectives 3

Bounding parameters are selected for DBAs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.1, p. 3.3-1

DBA analyses consider only safety-related SSCs for mitigation. AOO, DBE, and BDBE analyses take into account the expected response of all SSCs, regardless of safety classification. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.2.1, p. 3.3-3

AOO, DBE, and BDBE transients use either the conservative DBA approach or a best-estimate plus uncertainty approach. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.2.1, p. 3.3-3

The assumption that a function fails in an LBE description does not necessarily mean the failure resulted from the initiating event. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.4.1, p. 3.3-4

Knowledge check: Initial conditions and end states

Objective 4PL-03-K4

What peak cladding temperature limit defines the safe shutdown end state?

The answer is A. The safe shutdown state requires peak cladding temperature below 1157 F (625 C), stable or decreasing. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.4.3, p. 3.3-7

Objective 4PL-03-K5

What full power core inlet temperature is assumed in the LBE analyses?

The answer is B. Full power assumes 100% power, 100% flow, and 360.1 C core inlet. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.4.2, p. 3.3-7

Risk significance

Objectives 5

LBEs are risk-significant if the site boundary dose exceeds 2.5 mrem over 30 days and the frequency of the dose is within one percent of the F-C target. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.5.1, p. 3.5-1

BDBEs are high-consequence if the dose exceeds 10 CFR 50.34 criteria. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.5.1, p. 3.5-1

The major accident

Objectives 7

The major accident is a deterministic analysis that meets 10 CFR 50.34(a)(1)(ii)(D). PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.3, p. 3.3-4

For Kemmerer Unit 1, the EAB and LPZ are the same boundary, a 0.25-mile radius from the Reactor Building centerpoint. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.3, p. 3.3-4

The DBA with the maximum core damage and associated in-vessel release was selected as the major accident. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.3, p. 3.3-4

Summary

  • PRA-derived event sequences are sorted into AOOs, DBEs, and BDBEs by frequency. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8, p. 3.8-1
  • DBAs credit only safety-related SSCs, while other LBEs consider all SSCs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.2.1, p. 3.3-2
  • Safe shutdown requires peak cladding temperature below 625 C and stable or decreasing. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.4.3, p. 3.3-7

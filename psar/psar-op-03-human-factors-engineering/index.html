Goal and scope

Objectives 1

The goal is an integrated program that reduces risks and consequences influenced by human interactions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.1.1, p. 11.2-1

An HSI is any region or point at which a person interacts with a system, equipment, or component. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.1.2, p. 11.2-3

The scope includes both a part-task and a full-scope simulator. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.1.2, p. 11.2-3

HSI design aims for safe, efficient, reliable operator performance in normal, abnormal, and accident conditions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.1.9, p. 11.2-9

The HFE Style Guide is based on NUREG-0700. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.1.9, p. 11.2-10

HFE rigor is graded, with more emphasis on interactions that are safety critical or hazardous. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.3, p. 11.2-18

Operating experience and human actions

Objectives 3

OER lessons improve the design early in the process. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.2, p. 11.2-14

It includes sodium-cooled facilities such as the Fast Flux Test Facility. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.2.2.2, p. 11.2-16

Recognized issues include Three Mile Island, Chernobyl, and Fukushima Daiichi. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.2.2.4, p. 11.2-17

Plant personnel interviews supplement OE. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.2.2.5, p. 11.2-17

Human reliability analysis looks at the potential for, and mechanisms of, human error. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.3, p. 11.2-19

The minimum set of controls, displays, and alarms comes from emergency procedure guidelines and important actions in the PRA. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.1.12, p. 11.2-11

Verification and validation

Objectives 4

HF V&V has two major activities: HF verification and Integrated System Validation (ISV). PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.1.4, p. 11.2-7

A team independent of design development does it. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.1.4, p. 11.2-7

Task Support Verification checks that HSIs have what tasks need, and no unnecessary features. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.1.4, p. 11.2-7

ISV is an integrated, dynamic, performance-based test. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.1.4, p. 11.2-7

Human performance monitoring

Objectives 6

HPM links design HFE methods with monitoring during operation, and is fully developed at the OL stage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.7, p. 11.2-25

  • Keep safety standards as changes are made
  • Detect deterioration of task performance from latent or slow-developing issues
  • Assure the safety bases stay valid in operation

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.7.1, p. 11.2-25

  • Data collection
  • Importance screening
  • Event analysis to determine causes
  • Trend analysis
  • Corrective action development

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.7.2, p. 11.2-26

Collected data is anonymized to keep the program free of blame. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.2.7.2, p. 11.2-26

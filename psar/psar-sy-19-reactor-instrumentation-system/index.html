The RIS is the set of non-nuclear sensors in or near the reactor vessel. It measures pool temperature, level, and pump data, and has no displays or logic of its own. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.6.5, pp. 7.6-48 to 7.6-59

Terms with a dotted underline are PSAR acronyms. Hover, tap or press Enter on one to see its meaning.

Purpose and architecture

Objectives 1, 2, 3

The RIS measures selected parameters in or near the reactor vessel and sends signals to the RPS and NIC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.1, p. 7.6-48

A channel is a single sensor plus its cabling and signal amplification electronics. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.1, p. 7.6-48

The RIS has four redundant, independent divisions for parameters that give SR inputs to the RPS. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.1.1, p. 7.6-48

Signal splitters duplicate SR inputs and send them to the NIC as NSRST and NST signals. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.1.1, p. 7.6-48

No information displays are part of the RIS. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.3.3, p. 7.6-55

Testing and failure

Objectives 8, 10

SR portions can be tested and calibrated during power operation with no loss of SR functions at the system level. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.3.2, p. 7.6-54

SR equipment is designed for timely recognition, location, adjustment, repair, and replacement of malfunctions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.3.2, p. 7.6-54

Inputs to the NIC are electronically isolated from RPS inputs, so control failures do not impact the RPS. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.2.2, p. 7.6-53

Removing any RIS division will not impact protection or control functions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.2.2, p. 7.6-53

SR channels give the same outputs for the same inputs within a known cycle time. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.3.3, p. 7.6-56

Safety significance

Objectives 9, 10

Safety-significant RIS equipment is seismic class SCS1. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.3.1, p. 7.6-53

Safety-significant portions sit in the RXB and NCB substructures. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.1.2, p. 7.6-48

NCB equipment is in division-specific 3-hour fire rated rooms. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.2.2, p. 7.6-51

Summary

  • The RIS is a collection of channels in or near the vessel, with four SR divisions where the RPS needs them. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.1.1, p. 7.6-48
  • It gives the RPS temperature, level, and PSP speed and current, and has no displays. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.3.3, p. 7.6-55
  • Signal splitters isolate control from protection, so NIC failures do not reach the RPS. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.5.2.2, p. 7.6-53

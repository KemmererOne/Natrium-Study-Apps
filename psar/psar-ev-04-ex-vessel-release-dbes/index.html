Objective 1EV-04-K1

Which DBE releases precipitated tritium?

The answer is A. RRS-ISPL-BL releases precipitated tritium from the SPS-I cold trap. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.3.1, p. 3.7-7

Source term assumptions

Objectives 1, 2

DBEKey assumption
SCG leaksInstantaneous release of the cover gas inventory into the HAA compartment PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.1.2, p. 3.7-6
SCG leaksFor 30 minutes the HAA exhausts at 400 cfm with NHV filtration, then NHV isolates and the HAA leaks to the RXB at 10 percent volume per day PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.1.2, p. 3.7-6
SPS cold trapRelease from one cold trap over 24 hours, with the RAB released instantaneously PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.3.2, p. 3.7-7
SPS-P in the RABAirborne activated sodium equal to 20 gallons of system fluid PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.5.2, p. 3.7-9
RWG to FHBVault discharges at 500 cfm with NHV filtration PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.6.2, p. 3.7-9
RWG to stackRelease through the vent stack with filtration over 10 minutes PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.7.2, p. 3.7-10

Most SPS piping slopes down toward the reactor, which minimizes potential leakage volume. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.5.2, p. 3.7-8

Knowledge check: Source term assumptions

Objective 1,2EV-04-K2

In the SCG leak DBEs, what happens at 30 minutes?

The answer is A. At 30 minutes the HAA NHV system is isolated and the HAA begins to leak to the RXB. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.1.2, p. 3.7-6

Objective 1,2EV-04-K3

Why is the SPS piping sloped toward the reactor?

The answer is D. Sloping minimizes the potential leakage volume from a break. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.5.2, p. 3.7-8

Knowledge check: Automatic protective action and chronology

Objective 3,5EV-04-K5

What stops the loss of primary sodium inventory in an SPS-P leak?

The answer is B. The SPS pump trips on low primary sodium level, terminating inventory loss. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.4.1, p. 3.7-8

Summary

  • These DBEs challenge radionuclide retention, with no fuel cladding failure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.1.1, p. 3.7-5
  • SPS-P leaks are stopped by the SPS pump trip on low primary sodium level. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.3.4.1, p. 3.7-8

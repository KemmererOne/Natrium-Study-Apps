Event initiators and assumptions

Objectives 1

BDBEs are less likely than DBEs and are not expected to occur in the life of the plant. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8, p. 3.8-1

BDBEs take into account the expected response of all SSCs regardless of safety classification. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8, p. 3.8-1

Knowledge check: Event initiators and assumptions

Objective 1EV-08-K1

What frequency band defines BDBEs?

The answer is C. Mean frequencies of 5x10^-7 to 1x10^-4 per plant-year are BDBEs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8, p. 3.8-1

Knowledge check: Scram backups

Objective 5,9EV-08-K2

An insufficient number of rods insert after a scram signal. What backs this up in the BDBE analysis?

The answer is A. A CRD driveline scram follow drives rods into the core. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8.1.2.1, p. 3.8-1

Report a problem
Objective 5,9EV-08-K3

The normal reactor scram fails. What scrams the reactor?

The answer is D. The alternative shunt trip circuit scrams the reactor. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8.1.3.1, p. 3.8-2

Heat removal fallback

Objectives 5, 9, 10

With non-passive IAC failed on both trains, the end state is safe shutdown with the IAC passive mode. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8.1.1.1, p. 3.8-1

With non-passive and passive IAC failed on both trains, RAC provides decay heat removal. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8.1.5.1, p. 3.8-2

Learn the order: IAC non-passive, then IAC passive, then RAC.

Where fuel performance limits are not met

Objectives 2

In DHP-LOOP-4, the fuel performance acceptance criteria are not met. Cladding fails, and a radionuclide release occurs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8.1.6.1, p. 3.8-3

Risk-significant BDBEs in the summary table include RFH-FDIV-2, RFH-FDEM-2, and RFH-FDPI-1. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.5.2, p. 3.5-6

Objective 2EV-08-K5

Which LOOP BDBE fails to meet the fuel performance acceptance criteria?

The answer is A. DHP-LOOP-4 uses the alternative shunt trip and cladding fails. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.8.1.6.1, p. 3.8-3

Radiation Protection Program and ALARA

Objectives 1

The RPP will be in place before radioactive material is received on-site. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 9.3.2, p. 10.1-1

It ensures 10 CFR 19.12 and 10 CFR 20 are met. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 9.3.2, p. 10.1-1

All personnel maintain ALARA practices, and may halt any operation they judge radiologically unsafe. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.1, p. 10.1-2

The ALARA program meets 10 CFR 20.1101 and follows RG 8.10. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.2, p. 10.2-1

Surveys and monitoring meet 10 CFR 20 Subpart F. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.1.1, p. 10.1-2

Air measurements and bioassays confirm internal exposures stay within 10 CFR 20 Subpart C limits. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.1.2, p. 10.1-2

Objective 1OP-06-K1

Who may halt a radiologically unsafe operation?

The answer is A. All personnel have this authority. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.1, p. 10.1-2

What is used to determine internal exposure?

The answer is C. Those two are named. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.1.2, p. 10.1-2

High and Very High Radiation Areas

Objectives 2

HRA and VHRA access follows RG 8.38. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.1.3, p. 10.1-2

  • Training of workers
  • Job planning
  • Pre-job surveys
  • Pre-job briefing
  • Radiation work permits or equivalent
  • Radiation protection coverage
  • Measuring and alarming dosimeters
  • Posting
  • Physical barriers
  • Locks

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.1.3, p. 10.1-2

Access to VHRAs is generally forbidden unless there is a sound operational or safety reason. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.1.3, p. 10.1-3

Design features for ALARA

Objectives 4

  • Reducing time spent in radiation areas
  • Reducing maintenance frequency
  • Improving access to components
  • Reducing production, distribution, and retention of activated products

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.3, p. 10.3-1

Shield assemblies absorb radial neutron leakage to limit activation of intermediate sodium. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.4.1, p. 10.4-1

The SPS removes cesium from the PHT using Reticulated Vitreous Carbon traps. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.4.2, p. 10.4-1

A central location remotely controls fuel handling equipment. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.4.3, p. 10.4-1

Negative pressure gradients carry contamination toward higher contamination areas. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 10.3, p. 10.3-2

Contamination control

Objectives 5

The design minimizes contamination and waste, and eases decommissioning, per 10 CFR 20.1406. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 9.2, p. 9.2-1

  • Minimization of leaks and spills
  • Adequate leak detection
  • Avoiding release from undetected leaks
  • Reducing need to decontaminate
  • Periodic review of practices

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 9.2, p. 9.2-1

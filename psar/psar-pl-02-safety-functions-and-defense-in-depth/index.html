Fundamental Safety Functions

Objectives 1

The FSFs ensure the cumulative risk objectives of regulatory dose criteria and Quantitative Health Objectives are met. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2, p. 1.3-1

"The FSFs are retaining radionuclides, controlling heat generation, and controlling heat removal." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2, p. 1.3-1
FSFHow it is performed
Retaining radionuclidesFunctional containment strategy PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2, p. 1.3-2
Controlling heat generationInsertion of control rods into the reactor core PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2, p. 1.3-2
Controlling heat removalActive and passive cooling systems PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2, p. 1.3-2

The heat generation and heat removal functions both serve the same barrier, as the next page shows.

Functional containment

Objectives 2

Functional containment employs diverse passive barriers. They begin at a radionuclide source and include all SSCs between that source and the environment. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.1, p. 1.3-2

  • Maintain at least one barrier beyond the cladding, piping, or vessel containing a source that can reliably withstand LBE and DBA conditions, with leakage specified by design or testing
  • Keep a minimum total number of required barriers between a source and the environment
  • Provide a framework for iteration between safety analysis, design, and PRA

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.1, p. 1.3-2

For the fuel, the cladding provides the first safety-related radionuclide retention barrier. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.1, p. 1.3-2

The Reactor Enclosure System is the next barrier. Its SR portion includes the reactor head, reactor vessel, and associated component interfaces. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.1, p. 1.3-2

The guard vessel provides an additional barrier outside the reactor head and reactor vessel SR boundary. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.1, p. 1.3-2

There are no penetrations through either of the two vessels. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.1, p. 1.3-3

A fuel assembly that fails during operation is sealed in an SR canister before storage in the Spent Fuel Pool. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.1, p. 1.3-2

Inherent features that support containment

Objectives 2

The high boiling point of sodium keeps the core covered by subcooled sodium at near atmospheric pressure during LBEs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.1, p. 1.3-3

The low operating pressure of the primary system and cover gas means there is no significant driving force to transport radionuclides. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.1, p. 1.3-3

Intermediate coolant pressure is greater than primary coolant pressure, so postulated intermediate leaks send sodium into the primary system rather than primary coolant into the intermediate system. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.1, p. 1.3-3

Controlling heat removal

Objectives 3

Two diverse residual heat removal systems, IAC and RAC, perform functions that satisfy this FSF. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.3, p. 1.3-4

ModeAir flowSodium flow in the IHT
IAC active mode 1IAC blowerForced flow from the Intermediate Sodium Pump PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.3, p. 1.3-4
IAC active mode 2IAC blowerNatural circulation PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.3, p. 1.3-4
IAC passive modeNatural draftNot stated here PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.3, p. 1.3-4

IAC is the primary means of heat removal during plant start-up, shutdown, and off-normal conditions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.3, p. 1.3-5

RAC uses natural circulation of air around the exterior of the guard vessel. It operates continuously, with no operator actions or moving parts required. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.3, p. 1.3-5

The five Defense Lines

Objectives 4

LinePurpose
DL1Prevent off-normal operation and AOOs PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1, p. 4.2-1
DL2Control abnormal operation, detect failures, and prevent DBEs PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1, p. 4.2-2
DL3Control DBEs within the analyzed design basis conditions and prevent BDBEs PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1, p. 4.2-2
DL4Control severe plant conditions and mitigate consequences of BDBEs PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1, p. 4.2-2
DL5Deploy adequate offsite protective actions and prevent adverse impact on public health and safety PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1, p. 4.2-2

DL1 and DL5 include programmatic elements and design features. DL2, DL3, and DL4 include the design functions needed to ensure performance of the FSFs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1, p. 4.2-2

Among DLs 2, 3, and 4, two independent and diverse DLs, to the extent practicable, can mitigate any AOO postulated initiating event. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1, p. 4.2-2

SARRDLs

Objectives 5

A SARRDL is a prescribed limit of radionuclide release for systems and components. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2, p. 1.3-1

  • The most limiting LBE does not exceed the siting dose limits at the EAB and LPZ
  • The 10 CFR 20.1301 annualized dose limits to the public are not exceeded at the EAB for normal operation and AOOs

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2, p. 1.3-1

Compliance comes from monitoring and controlling radionuclide inventory during normal operation, so the assumed inventory at the onset of an LBE is not exceeded. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2, p. 1.3-1

Evaluating defense-in-depth

Objectives 6

The three DID elements are plant capability, programmatic capability, and the integrated DID adequacy evaluation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.3, p. 1.3-5

  • Evaluation of LBE margin
  • Layers of defense evaluation
  • Evaluation of single feature reliance
  • Evaluation of prevention-mitigation balance

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2, p. 4.2-1

The frequency of all DBEs and BDBEs is maintained below 1x10^-2 and 1x10^-4 per plant-year, respectively. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1, p. 4.2-2

Eight risk-significant LBEs are identified. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1.2, p. 4.2-3

The single feature review found no dependence on a single feature across the layers for the risk-significant LBEs. One DBA was identified with dependence on a single barrier. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1.3, p. 4.2-4

An integrated decision-making process panel uses deterministic and probabilistic inputs to review DID adequacy. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.3, p. 4.2-5

The DID baseline to support plant operation will be established at the operating license stage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 4.2.1.1, p. 4.2-3

Summary

  • The FSFs are retaining radionuclides, controlling heat generation, and controlling heat removal. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2, p. 1.3-1
  • Functional containment uses diverse passive barriers, with the cladding first. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.1, p. 1.3-2
  • Scram is passive gravity insertion, backed by the driveline scram follow. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.2, p. 1.3-4
  • IAC has active and passive modes, and RAC runs continuously with no moving parts. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.3.2.3, p. 1.3-5

The NFP is four systems. Three are conventional fire protection. The fourth, NNA, deals with sodium, and sodium buildings are kept free of fire water. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.5.2, pp. 7.5-10 to 7.5-20

Purpose and the four systems

Objectives 1, 2

The primary purpose of the NFP is to mitigate the effects of fire within and around the NI facilities. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.1, p. 7.5-10

SystemJobClass
NFDDistributes fire water for hydrants and water-based suppressionNST PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.1, p. 7.5-10
NFSSuppresses and controls fire with water, clean agents, and chemical meansNST PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.1, p. 7.5-10
NFADetects smoke and fire, monitors suppression, notifies personnelNST PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.1, p. 7.5-10
NNADetects, collects, and contains sodium leaksNSRST PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.1, p. 7.5-10

NNA strategy is to mitigate the effects of a sodium fire once a leak has occurred. Leak prevention is outside its scope. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.1, p. 7.5-10

The Fire Protection Program and Fire Hazards Analysis will be provided at the operating license stage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3, p. 7.5-12

Fire water supply

Objectives 3, 4

Fire water comes from the Energy Island Fire Protection System (EFP). PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.1, p. 7.5-10

There are two fire water storage tanks with a minimum of 300,000 gallons each. Either can be refilled in 8 hours or less. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.1, p. 7.5-13

There are two fire pumps: a primary electric motor pump and a backup diesel engine driven pump. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.1, p. 7.5-13

A jockey pump keeps required pressure in normal conditions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.1, p. 7.5-13

The pumps have a 3-hour fire rated separation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.1, p. 7.5-13

Suppression and where water is kept out

Objectives 3, 4, 9

Water-based suppression is in buildings where sodium is not present. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.2, p. 7.5-13

The RXB and RAB contain sodium, are not sprinklered, and do not contain fire water. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.2, p. 7.5-13

The FHB contains sodium but has separated sprinklered areas, such as radwaste handling areas and truck bays. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.2, p. 7.5-13

Preaction systems are used where water-filled piping is not desirable. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.2, p. 7.5-13

Clean agent systems serve areas where water would be detrimental. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.2, p. 7.5-14

Four independent clean agent systems serve the NCB substructure, one for each RPS division. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.2, p. 7.5-14

Class D extinguishers are in areas containing sodium. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.2, p. 7.5-14

Detection and alarm

Objectives 3, 5

NFA has initiation devices, notification appliances, control equipment, and wiring. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.3, p. 7.5-14

Each building has its own Fire Alarm Control Panel. Panels are networked to the NFA workstation in the MCR. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.5, p. 7.5-17

NFA and NNA have internal batteries for when power is not available. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.3, p. 7.5-14

Normal NFP operation is standby. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.5, p. 7.5-17

When smoke is detected in a duct, the NHV air handling unit shuts down and local smoke dampers close. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.5, p. 7.5-17

NFA also has trouble and supervisory modes. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.5, p. 7.5-17

Sprinklers activate individually at their temperature, and clean agent systems activate on a signal from the NFA. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.5, p. 7.5-17

Sodium leak strategy

Objectives 3, 4

NNA detects leaked sodium, collects it, and drains it to catch pans, while giving operators early notice. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-14

The overall strategy uses passive features and adds active ones only where necessary. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-15

FeatureDescription
CellRoom with limited leak rate and known oxygen volume, either inerted or de facto inerted after a leak PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-15
Guard pipeFully redundant pipe that acts as a full secondary pressure boundary PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-15
Guard enclosureLike a guard pipe, with a drainpipe to a collection location PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-15
ClamshellGuard enclosure placed where piping inspections are expected PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-15
Catch panSteel-lined volume that permanently retains a design-basis leak volume PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16
Suppression deckApplied to catch pans where oxygen volume cannot be limited PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16

Primary SPS cells in the RAB are inerted. IHT cells in the RAB are not. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-15

Intermediate sodium leaks in the HAA must be fully contained and removed from the HAA. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-15

Containment features are sized for the maximum leak volume plus 20%, and catch pan calculations use 25%. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16

Sodium leak detection

Objectives 5, 6, 8

Detectors are in leak mitigative features, at vulnerable valves, and inside catch pans. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16

Detectors are generally conductivity based with a self-test function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16

Smoke detection is used where sodium reactions may occur, such as IAC stacks. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16

Leak detector activation alarms in the MCR through the NIC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16

FunctionSignal pathAction
DL4-RR3aNNA to AMCAMC closes the SPS-P supply valve PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.2, p. 7.5-12
DL4-RR3bNNA to AMCAMC trips SPS-P pumps PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.2, p. 7.5-12
DL4-RR3cNNA to UMCUMC closes NHV dampers PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.2, p. 7.5-12

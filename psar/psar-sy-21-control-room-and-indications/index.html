This lesson covers where operators work: the MCR, the Remote Shutdown Complex, and the emergency response facilities. There are no safety-related operator actions. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.6.7, pp. 7.6-69 to 7.6-74

Terms with a dotted underline are PSAR acronyms. Hover, tap or press Enter on one to see its meaning.

Learning objectives

1 State the purpose of the Main Control Room and Remote Shutdown Complex.

Taught in: Purpose and location

2 Identify the Main Control Room and Remote Shutdown Complex boundaries and interfaces.

Taught in: Purpose and location

3 Identify the major components of the Main Control Room and Remote Shutdown Complex.

Taught in: MCR layout, Remote Shutdown Complex

4 Explain Main Control Room and Remote Shutdown Complex operation.

Not in PSAR Rev 1. This lesson does not teach it.

5 Describe the Main Control Room and Remote Shutdown Complex controls and indications.

Taught in: MCR layout, Safety Parameter Display System and alarms

6 Explain the Main Control Room and Remote Shutdown Complex automatic features and interlocks.

Not in PSAR Rev 1. This lesson does not teach it.

7 Apply operating procedures for the Main Control Room and Remote Shutdown Complex.

Taught in: Remote Shutdown Complex

8 Diagnose abnormal conditions in the Main Control Room and Remote Shutdown Complex.

Taught in: Safety Parameter Display System and alarms, Remote Shutdown Complex

9 Explain the safety significance of the Main Control Room and Remote Shutdown Complex.

Taught in: Purpose and location, Emergency response facilities and safety significance

10 Address technical specifications and administrative controls for the Main Control Room and Remote Shutdown Complex.

Taught in: Emergency response facilities and safety significance

Purpose and location

Objectives 1, 2, 9

The control room and indications satisfy PDC 19 and the emergency plan. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7, p. 7.6-69

The MCR is in the NSRST NCB superstructure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1, p. 7.6-69

There are no safety-related operator actions required. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1, p. 7.6-69

NHV controls the environment and protects habitability for radiological, sodium aerosol, and chemical hazards. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1, p. 7.6-69

Separation between SR and other equipment is achieved mainly with relays, and an FMEA will confirm that failures cannot inhibit the safety function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.1, p. 7.6-69

MCR layout

Objectives 3, 5

WorkstationProvides
RPS workstationFour SVDUs, one per division, and manual scram and ESF controls PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.2, p. 7.6-70
GVDSWall-mounted VDUs with key parameters and alarms PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.2, p. 7.6-70
NI and EI operator workstationsControls and indications through NIC and EIC PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.2, p. 7.6-71
Supervisor workstationIndications with no controls PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.2, p. 7.6-71
Communications workstationListed in the MCR equipment PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.2, p. 7.6-70

There are two workstations for the NI portion and two for the EI portion. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.2, p. 7.6-71

Each desk has four VDUs, a hardwired mouse, and a hardwired keyboard. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.2, p. 7.6-71

Division A and B SVDUs also display their PAM variables. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.2, p. 7.6-70

RPS SVDUs and manual controls are classified NSRST. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.2, p. 7.6-70

Safety Parameter Display System and alarms

Objectives 5, 8

  • Variables over the full range expected
  • Data trending
  • Indication when limits are approached or exceeded
  • Safety system status

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.3, p. 7.6-72

It includes displays that help staff run emergency procedures, severe accident procedures, and site response. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.3, p. 7.6-72

Alarms indicate deviations from normal limits, loss of safety system availability, and unplanned unavailability of standby equipment. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.3, p. 7.6-72

Remote Shutdown Complex

Objectives 3, 7, 8

If the MCR cannot be inhabited, operators evacuate to the RSC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.2, p. 7.6-72

The RSC is in the Division B RPS Vault, DCS, and CRD cabinet room. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.2, p. 7.6-72

It is NSRST and sits in the SR NCB substructure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.2, p. 7.6-72

A manually started bottled air injection system supports three operators with a 72-hour mission time. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.2, p. 7.6-72

SCBA and hazardous materials suits are stored there for leaving the area. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.2, p. 7.6-72

An intermediate chamber sized for one person allows entry and exit. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.2, p. 7.6-72

Two RPS SVDUs show Divisions A and B, including their PAM variables. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.2.1, p. 7.6-73

The location gives access to equipment able to trip all RTBs and ESF components. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.2.1, p. 7.6-73

Trip equipment is also in the PSP and ISP trip breaker rooms, reached with SCBA and suits. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.2.1, p. 7.6-73

Emergency response facilities and safety significance

Objectives 9, 10

Primary and Backup ERF details will be described at the OL stage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.3, p. 7.6-73

The Primary and Backup ERFs receive data from the NIC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.3, p. 7.6-73

The HFE program applies to the MCR and RSC design. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.2.2, p. 7.6-73

MCR indications comply with the technologically relevant portions of 10 CFR 50.34(f). PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.3, p. 7.6-71

Summary

  • The MCR is NSRST, and no safety-related operator actions are required. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1, p. 7.6-69
  • Four SVDUs and manual controls sit at the RPS workstation, each SVDU for its own division. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.1.2, p. 7.6-70
  • If the MCR is lost, the RSC gives three operators 72 hours on bottled air and access to trip equipment. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.7.2, p. 7.6-72

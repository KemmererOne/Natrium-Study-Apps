The NUP and NDC give the Nuclear Island uninterruptible AC and DC power. They are not safety related. The safety functions do not depend on them, which is a key design point. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.7.1, pp. 7.7-1 to 7.7-10

Terms with a dotted underline are PSAR acronyms. Hover, tap or press Enter on one to see its meaning.

Purpose and classification

Objectives 1, 9

NUP is the uninterruptible AC system, and NDC is the DC system. Both power NI systems in all modes. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.1, p. 7.7-1

Failures of the NUP or NDC do not affect the plant's ability to perform SR functions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.1, p. 7.7-1

Portions in Divisions A, B, C, and D are NSRST, because they power NSRST SSC functions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.1, p. 7.7-1

Electrical systems are otherwise NST. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7, p. 7.7-1

NUP design

Objectives 3, 4

The NUP has four independent divisions, one per RPS division. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.1, p. 7.7-4

  • 120 VAC single-phase inverter with integrated static transfer switch (STS)
  • 480/120 VAC bypass Regulating Voltage Transformer (RVT)
  • Two 120 VAC panelboards, one UPS-backed and one not
  • Remote Manual Bypass Switch (RMBS)

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.1, p. 7.7-4

In normal operation, the inverter takes 250 VDC from its switchboard and makes regulated 120 VAC for the UPS panelboard. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.1, p. 7.7-4

The non-UPS panelboard feeds the same loads as a backup. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.1, p. 7.7-4

Each RVT takes 480 VAC from the same NST SDG-backed MCC as that division's normal battery charger. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.1, p. 7.7-4

NDC design

Objectives 3, 4

The NDC has four independent 250 VDC divisions, one per RPS division. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.2, p. 7.7-5

Each division has a battery bank, a Battery Transfer Switch (BTS), a normal switchboard, and access to two chargers. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.2, p. 7.7-5

Each charger can supply its loads while recharging its battery from design minimum to full within 24 hours. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.2, p. 7.7-5

Swing chargers are shared between Divisions A and B, and between C and D. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.2, p. 7.7-6

DivisionsBattery duty
A and B72 hours PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.2, p. 7.7-6
C and D2 hours PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.2, p. 7.7-6

Batteries are sealed Valve-Regulated Lead-Acid. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.2, p. 7.7-6

Automatic transfers and abnormal response

Objectives 6, 8

If an inverter fails or there is an output overload or short, the STS automatically transfers UPS loads to the RVT. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.1, p. 7.7-5

For inverter maintenance, loads go to the RVT using the RMBS. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.1, p. 7.7-5

On loss of offsite power, NST SDGs start automatically and power their MCCs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.1, p. 7.7-4

Batteries supply uninterrupted power until the SDGs come online. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.1, p. 7.7-4

Loss of one NLV MCC loses one charger until the swing charger is on line, and the battery carries the loads. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.2, p. 7.7-5

If input voltage and frequency are out of limits, chargers shut down and isolate the NDC, then return automatically. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.5, p. 7.7-8

Charger DC overvoltage protection shuts the charger down before inverter overvoltage protection acts. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.5, p. 7.7-8

The NDC is ungrounded. Ground detection relays send a common trouble alarm to the UMC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.2, p. 7.7-6

Indications and testing

Objectives 5, 10

Voltmeters and ammeters give continuous monitoring locally and through the UMC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.5, p. 7.7-7

A protection or monitoring device operation sends a common trouble signal to the UMC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.5, p. 7.7-7

A battery monitoring system watches cell temperature, cell resistance, charging current, float voltage, and room temperature. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.2, p. 7.7-6

The BTS allows battery equalization and discharge testing. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.2, p. 7.7-6

Safety significance

Objectives 9, 10

NUP and NDC support NSRST functions, including scram follow, seismic trip, pump trips, and PAM. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.2, p. 7.7-3

Safety-significant components are in the NCB substructure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.2, p. 7.7-2

Independence comes from physical separation and electrical isolation, so a single failure affects only one division. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.7.1.3.1, p. 7.7-5

  • Design Reliability Assurance Program
  • Equipment Qualification Program
  • Post Construction Inspection, Testing, and Analysis Program
  • Quality Assurance Program Description

The PHT moves core heat to the intermediate loops. It has two pumps, two heat exchangers, and three sodium pools, and it keeps working by natural circulation when the pumps stop. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.1.3, pp. 7.1-50 to 7.1-74

Purpose and boundaries

Objectives 1, 2

The primary function of the PHT is to transport heat from the reactor core to the IHT. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1, p. 7.1-50

A safety-significant function is to keep primary sodium temperatures within limits that preclude damage to the reactor, fuel, or internals. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1, p. 7.1-50

The PHT includes two IHXs, two PSPs, and three sodium pools inside the RV. PSP flanges, IHX flanges, and PSP motors are above the reactor head. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1, p. 7.1-50

PSP isolation circuit breakers are in the Nuclear Island Control Building, and the PSP adjustable speed drives are in the Nuclear Island Electrical Building. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1, p. 7.1-50

Primary Sodium Pumps

Objectives 3

The PSPs are two identical vertical centrifugal pumps on opposite sides of the RV. They draw from the cold pool and discharge into the core inlet plenum. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.1, p. 7.1-60

The pump shaft is sealed near the support flange to prevent sodium and cover gas leakage, and the seal also limits air and moisture leakage into the RV. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.1, p. 7.1-60

ParameterNominal value
Flow at full power40,000 gpm PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.3.5, p. 7.1-67
Pump head at full power300 ft PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.3.5, p. 7.1-67
Coastdown halving time3.5 to 5 s PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.3.5, p. 7.1-67
Design temperature1,050 F PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.3.5, p. 7.1-67

Intermediate Heat Exchangers

Objectives 3

The IHXs are vertical, counter-flow, single-pass, straight-tube heat exchangers with a floating lower head. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.3.1, p. 7.1-62

Primary sodium is on the shell side and intermediate sodium is on the tube side. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.3.1, p. 7.1-63

ParameterNominal value
Rated capacity425 MWth each PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.3.5, p. 7.1-69
Primary inlet and outlet950 F and 680 F PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.3.5, p. 7.1-69
Intermediate inlet and outlet620 F and 910 F PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.3.5, p. 7.1-69

Normal operation

Objectives 4

During normal operation, in Modes 1, 2, 3, and 4, the PSPs drive flow from the cold pool, through the core to the hot pool, and through the IHX to the cold pool. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1.2, p. 7.1-58

The PSPs are the only PHT components that need operator inputs to change state in normal operation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.3, p. 7.1-61

ConditionPSP operation
Full powerFull flow PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.3, p. 7.1-61
Refueling and shutdownLow flow PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.3, p. 7.1-61
Extended shutdownVariable flow, to provide heat that keeps sodium liquid PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.3, p. 7.1-61
StartupRaise sodium to hot zero power temperature, then reduce to minimum and increase with power PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.3, p. 7.1-61

Response to runback, scram, and LOOP

Objectives 4, 6

EventPSP response
Power runbackBoth PSPs gradually reduce flow to follow reactor power. Power-to-flow ratio stays at or below one PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1.2, p. 7.1-58
Reactor scramPSPs ramp down on a predefined curve to minimum flow PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.3, p. 7.1-61
Loss of offsite powerCoastdown starts immediately when power is lost PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.3, p. 7.1-62

After a LOOP, pumps go to minimum flow if power returns from the standby diesel generators. If not, flow reduces to natural circulation rates. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1.2, p. 7.1-59

During an LBE, the PSPs are automatically controlled by the NIC or the RPS. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.3, p. 7.1-61

Automatic trip and natural circulation

Objectives 6

PSP trip function DL3-HR2 uses a combination of low neutron flux signal, an existing scram signal, and cold pool temperature at the high high setpoint. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1.1, p. 7.1-56

In a PSP trip, rotational inertia gives coastdown flow for a smooth transition to natural circulation without flow reversal in the fuel channels. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1.1, p. 7.1-56

A natural flow path exists through the PSPs when the shaft is stopped. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.3, p. 7.1-62

During natural circulation the flow path is unchanged. If heat transfer to the IHT is not available, all decay heat goes to RAC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1, p. 7.1-51

Safety significance

Objectives 9, 10

No operator actions are required to implement PHT SR functions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1.2, p. 7.1-58

  • DL3-HR1: PSP coastdown
  • DL3-HR2: PSP trip on high high primary sodium temperature
  • DL3-HR5: natural circulation of sodium

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1.1, p. 7.1-56

PSP isolation circuit breakers are designed to permit periodic testing. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1.1, p. 7.1-55

High point vents are not required to maintain core cooling. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1.1, p. 7.1-57

The answer is A. No operator actions are required to implement PHT SR functions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1.2, p. 7.1-58

Summary

  • PSPs take suction from the cold pool and discharge to the core inlet plenum. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.2.1, p. 7.1-60
  • PSP inertia gives a smooth transition to natural circulation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.1.1, p. 7.1-56
  • IHX leakage goes from intermediate to primary, seen as expansion tank level down and hot pool level up. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.3.3, p. 7.1-63

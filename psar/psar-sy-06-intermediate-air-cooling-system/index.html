IAC is the normal way to reject reactor heat at low power and shutdown. Two trains of air heat exchangers sit in the yard, with three modes from fully powered to fully passive. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.2.2, pp. 7.2-13 to 7.2-21

Purpose

Objectives 1

IAC transfers heat from the intermediate sodium to the atmosphere. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.1, p. 7.2-13

IAC is the primary and preferred means of reactor heat removal during low power and shutdown conditions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.1, p. 7.2-13

It also supports start-up and shutdown transitions to and from NSS cooling. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.3, p. 7.2-16

Components and boundaries

Objectives 2, 3

IAC has two equal capacity trains. Each has an AHX, air stack, air blower, dampers, and air heater in an ASE structure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.1, p. 7.2-13

The two ASEs are in the yard on either side of the RAB. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.1, p. 7.2-13

An AHX is in series within each intermediate loop. Sodium is on the tube side and air on the shell side. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.1, p. 7.2-13

The ASEs have sodium catch pans and leak detection below the AHXs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.1, p. 7.2-13

Operating modes

Objectives 4

ModeSodium flowAir flowUse
ActiveForced, by ISPsForced, by blowersNormal operation cooling PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.3, p. 7.2-16
BlowerNatural circulationForced, by blowersOff-normal, when ISPs are unavailable PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.3, p. 7.2-17
PassiveNatural circulationNatural convectionEmergency cooling PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.3, p. 7.2-17

IAC is shut down and in standby during Mode 1. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.3, p. 7.2-16

A single train in Active Mode or Blower Mode provides the required heat removal for the limiting decay heat load. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.3, p. 7.2-17

In Passive Mode, the limiting decay heat load needs two trains in operation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.3, p. 7.2-17

Dampers and automatic behavior

Objectives 4, 6

Air dampers above and below the AHXs are normally closed in Mode 1 to limit heat transfer. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.1, p. 7.2-13

These dampers fail open on loss of power. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.1, p. 7.2-13

Blower discharge dampers are normally open and fail open. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.1, p. 7.2-13

No automatic actuations or manual actions are needed for Passive Mode, and no electrical power is needed to start natural convection airflow. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.3, p. 7.2-17

Sodium leak and fire protection

Objectives 8

The ASE diverts leakage to collection points and minimizes the surface area of leaked sodium exposed to air. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.2, p. 7.2-14

There are no water-based suppression systems and no fire water piping in the ASE. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.2, p. 7.2-14

Weather caps limit water intrusion into the ASE. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.2, p. 7.2-15

Safety significance

Objectives 9, 10

DL4-HR1 IAC passive mode operation is NSRST. It removes heat when the ISP and RAC are unavailable. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.2, p. 7.2-16

Passive Mode is the safety-significant defense-in-depth backup to the SR RAC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.3, p. 7.2-17

ASE, air stack, AHX, blower, heater, dampers, screens, and stack cap are NSRST. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.5, p. 7.2-19

Summary

  • IAC has two trains and three modes: Active, Blower, and Passive. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.1, p. 7.2-13
  • AHX dampers are closed in Mode 1 and fail open on loss of power. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.1, p. 7.2-13
  • Passive Mode needs no power and no actions, and is the DID backup to RAC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.2.3, p. 7.2-17

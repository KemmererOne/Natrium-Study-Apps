The XIS measures neutron leakage outside the core and turns it into a power signal for the RPS and NIC. It is analog, with one detector per division. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.6.4, pp. 7.6-36 to 7.6-47

Purpose and detectors

Objectives 1, 3

The XIS senses neutron flux leakage from the core during fuel movement, startup, power operations, shutdown, and accident conditions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1, p. 7.6-36

Neutron leakage is proportional to reactor flux, so it can be converted to a power indication. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1, p. 7.6-36

It uses a High Temperature Fission Chamber with a high voltage probe. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1, p. 7.6-36

The signal is converted to source, wide, and power range signals. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1, p. 7.6-36

There is one detector per division, four in total, each in its own drywell. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1, p. 7.6-36

The drywells are on the outside perimeter of the Core Barrel Structure outer barrel. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1, p. 7.6-36

Architecture and locations

Objectives 2, 4

Each XIS division is separate and independent, and the four divisions match the four RPS divisions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1.1, p. 7.6-36

Each XIS division interfaces with the corresponding RPS division only. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1.1, p. 7.6-37

XIS signals sent to the RPS reach the plant data bus through the RPS data gateway. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1.1, p. 7.6-37

NIC subsystems such as RMC and CTC get isolated hardwired signals from the XIS. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1.1, p. 7.6-37

Electronics enclosuresNCB substructure PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1.2, p. 7.6-37
Audible count rate monitorsMCR and FHCR PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1.2, p. 7.6-37

Indications and calibration

Objectives 5, 8

XIS status and data display through the RPS and NIC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.4, p. 7.6-45

NST audible count rate devices in the MCR and FHCR give source range indication by sound. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.4, p. 7.6-46

Power range and wide range channels need periodic calorimetric adjustments. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.3.2, p. 7.6-44

These align XIS readouts with power determined from core thermal power calculations. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.3.2, p. 7.6-44

Electronics modules perform continuous self-diagnostics, and a critical fault places all outputs in the safe state. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.2.2, p. 7.6-42

Any division can be out for testing with no loss of safety function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.2.2, p. 7.6-41

XIS channels are included in the overall bypass schemes for Maintenance Bypass. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.3.2, p. 7.6-44

Safety significance

Objectives 9, 10

The XIS is an SR system supporting SR, NSRST, and NST functions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.2, p. 7.6-37

XIS components are seismic class SCS1. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.3, p. 7.6-43

The XIS is currently an analog design with no software in SR functions, so there are no credible common-cause failures as required by IEEE 603. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.3.5, p. 7.6-45

Power comes from four NSRST divisions of the NUP. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.3.3, p. 7.6-45

NCB equipment is in division-specific 3-hour fire rated rooms. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.2.2, p. 7.6-40

Summary

  • Four fission chambers in drywells outside the core barrel feed four matched RPS divisions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1, p. 7.6-36
  • Signals go to the RPS for protection and isolated hardwire to the NIC for control. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.1.1, p. 7.6-37
  • The XIS is analog, SR, and SCS1, and calorimetric adjustments keep it aligned with true power. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.4.3.2, p. 7.6-44

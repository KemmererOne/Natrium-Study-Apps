Purpose and components

Objectives 1, 2, 3

The FHI manipulates core assembly positions during Mode 4 refueling and maintains the functional containment boundary when installed. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-40

  • Shuffle assemblies in the reactor vessel
  • Move assemblies to or from In-Vessel Storage
  • Receive assemblies from the FHE
  • Discharge assemblies to the FHE

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-40

ComponentRole
IVTMMulti-axis pantograph machine on the RPA that moves assemblies in the vessel PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-40
RPDRotates and restricts motion of the RPA PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-41
RPJLifts the RPA off its static interface so it can rotate on its bearing PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-41
FTLVertical lift that raises and lowers assemblies in a CAP PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-41
FTLSPPlug in the FTL bore that is part of the boundary while the FTL is installed or removed PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-42
IVDCLocal control system for the IVTM and RPD PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-41

In-Vessel Transfer Machine

Objectives 3, 4

The IVTM, with RPA rotation and IVDC motion control, moves assemblies between the core, In-Vessel Storage, and the FTL. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-40

A mechanical grapple grips the handling socket at the top of a core assembly. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-40

It handles one assembly at a time and keeps the assembly vertical. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-40

Control shafts run from the drive assembly in the HAA into the vessel. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-40

It is installed at the start of an outage in Mode 4, and removed in Mode 4. When out, a shield plug replaces it. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-40

If the IVTM is dropped in installation or removal, its nozzle flange contacts the RES structure, which keeps it out of the core. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-45

The grapple finger is in the research and development program. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-40

Fuel Transfer Lift

Objectives 3, 4

The FTL moves one assembly at a time, in a CAP, into and out of the reactor. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-41

FTL positionInterfaces with
RaisedEVHM grapple PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-41
LoweredIVTM grapple PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-41

The FTL is installed in the FTL nozzle at the start of a refueling outage and is not installed in non-outage periods. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-41

The FTL extends into the reactor, below the top of the core. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-46

FTL lifts are not critical lifts, but the lifting mechanisms have the enhanced features of critical lifts. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-46

Keeping the boundary during refueling

Objectives 2, 4, 8

IVTM and FTL components that form the boundary are SR. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-46

Static seals are between stationary parts, and dynamic seals are between shafts and their supports. All seals are redundant, with a primary and a secondary seal. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-46

Each volume between seals gets pressurized argon barrier gas from the SCG, slightly above reactor cover gas pressure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-46

This pressure keeps radionuclides from escaping if a seal fails. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-46

The FHC and IVDC monitor seal barrier gas. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-46

StepBoundary action
1FTLSP seals the inner bore when the FTL is installed PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-47
2CATT and FFV are installed and the FTLSP is removed PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-47
3CATT and FFV extend the boundary up to the refueling floor PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-47
4After refueling, the FTLSP is reinstalled and the FFV and CATT are removed PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-47

The FTLSP uses a redundant pair of static seals with argon barrier gas between them. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-42

How does barrier gas pressure compare to reactor cover gas?

What is done just after the CATT and FFV are installed?

Report a problem
Objective 2,4,8SY-12-K9

What monitors the seal barrier gas?

The answer is D. The seal barrier gas is monitored by the FHC and IVDC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.3, p. 7.3-46

Controls and interlocks

Objectives 5, 6

The IVDC turns FHC commands into motions of the IVTM and RPD. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-41

The IVDC enforces interlocks and dynamically updated safe operating zones of the IVTM. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-41

Control ranges from remote automatic through the FHC to manual in off-normal operations. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.1, p. 7.3-41

IVTM and FTL sensors report position, load, and status to the FHC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.5, p. 7.3-47

The FHC is NST and uses PMC human-machine interfaces in the FHB control room. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.3.3.5, p. 7.3-48

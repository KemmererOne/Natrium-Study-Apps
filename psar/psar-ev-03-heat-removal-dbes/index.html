Knowledge check: Event initiators and assumptions

Objective 1EV-03-K1

When is a heat removal upset not classified as an LBE?

The answer is D. A completed runback that prevents a scram setpoint from being reached means the event is not an LBE. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.4.1.1, p. 3.3-5

Event chronology

Objectives 3, 5

StepLoss of heat sink with RAC (DHS-ISTL-2)
1Heat removal is lost from one intermediate loop at full power PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.1.1, p. 3.7-2
2Reactor scrams on high high cold pool temperature PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.1.1, p. 3.7-2
3PSPs and ISPs trip on high high primary sodium temperature PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.1.1, p. 3.7-2
4Non-passive and passive IAC fail on both trains PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.1.3.1, p. 3.7-2
5Safe shutdown, with RAC removing decay heat PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.2.1, p. 3.7-3
StepIHX tube break (IPI-IHEL-BL)
1Tube break puts intermediate sodium into the PHT, and heat removal is lost from one loop PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.6.1, p. 3.7-4
2Reactor scrams on high high primary sodium level, and the ISPs trip on the same signal PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.6.1, p. 3.7-4
3Safe shutdown, with the unaffected IAC train in non-passive mode PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.6.1, p. 3.7-4

Knowledge check: Event chronology

Objective 3,5EV-03-K3

Which signal scrams the reactor in the loss of heat sink DBE?

The answer is A. The reactor scrams on high high cold pool temperature. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.1.1, p. 3.7-2

Report a problem
Objective 3,5EV-03-K4

Which signal scrams the reactor in the full power IHX tube break?

The answer is B. Intermediate sodium enters the primary, and the scram is on high high primary sodium level. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.6.1, p. 3.7-4

Report a problem
Objective 3,5EV-03-K5

In the Energy Island transient DBE, what happens to the runback?

The answer is C. A runback may be initiated, but a scram occurs before the runback is complete. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.3.1, p. 3.7-3

Knowledge check: Key parameter trends

Objective 4EV-03-K6

Heat removal from the primary suddenly increases. What does reactor power do before a runback or scram?

The answer is C. Sodium temperature falls, which causes an increase in reactor power. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.4.1.1, p. 3.3-5

Report a problem
Objective 4EV-03-K7

Heat removal from the primary decreases. Which pair is correct?

The answer is A. A decrease in heat removal raises primary sodium temperature, which decreases reactor power. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.4.1.2, p. 3.3-5

Credited equipment and failed functions

Objectives 8, 9

In the loss of heat sink with RAC, the thermal capacity of the piping, components, and intermediate sodium in the remaining loop is assumed to be available. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.2.1, p. 3.7-3

In the shutdown IAC train loss, the unaffected train provides decay heat removal in passive mode after non-passive cooling fails on that train. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.5.1, p. 3.7-4

The matching DBAs credit RAC for decay heat removal. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.2.1, p. 3.7-3

End state and operator role

Objectives 10, 12

The shutdown IAC event can end safe and stable at low power, or in safe shutdown. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.5.1, p. 3.7-4

The safe and stable end state applies if the transient does not result in a reactor scram. It requires K-effective of 1.0 and heat removal equal to heat production. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.4.3, p. 3.3-7

Objective 10,12EV-03-K9

Which end state applies when a transient does not cause a scram?

The answer is D. If the transient does not result in a scram, the safe and stable end state is used. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.4.3, p. 3.3-7

Summary

  • Heat removal upsets change sodium temperature first, and reactivity feedback then moves power the opposite way. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.4.1.1, p. 3.3-5
  • Loss of heat sink scrams on high high cold pool temperature. An IHX tube break scrams on high high primary sodium level. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.7.2.1.1, p. 3.7-2
  • A completed runback that prevents a scram setpoint means the event is not an LBE. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.3.4.1.1, p. 3.3-5

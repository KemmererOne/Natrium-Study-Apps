The IHT carries heat from the IHXs to the salt system at power and to the air heat exchangers at low power and shutdown. It has two loops, and either pumped or natural circulation flow. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.1.4, pp. 7.1-75 to 7.1-85

Purpose

Objectives 1

The IHT transports heat to the NSS for electrical power generation and energy storage during normal operation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.1, p. 7.1-75

It transports heat to the IAC for rejection to the atmosphere during start-up and shutdown, as the normal and preferred means of reactor cooling. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.1, p. 7.1-75

On loss of pumped flow, the IHT provides natural circulation for decay heat removal through the IAC AHXs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.1, p. 7.1-75

Components and boundaries

Objectives 2, 3

Each of the two loops has an ISP, an array of SHXs, and interconnecting piping. Each loop connects to one IHX and one IAC AHX. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.1, p. 7.1-75

Supporting equipment includes expansion tanks and a drain subsystem. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.1, p. 7.1-75

Loop piping and components are in the Reactor Auxiliary Building, except piping in the Reactor Building to the IHX nozzles and yard piping to the AHXs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.1, p. 7.1-75

The expansion tank interfaces with the SCG for an inert cover gas and overpressure protection, and with the SPS for sodium processing. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.3, p. 7.1-81

IHT piping in the HAA has a continuous leak guard as a secondary barrier. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.3, p. 7.1-82

Operation and pressure relationships

Objectives 4

ISP speed controls loop flow, through an adjustable speed drive that varies frequency and voltage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.1, p. 7.1-75

IAC Active Mode uses ISP flow. When ISPs are not available, Blower Mode and Passive Mode use natural circulation of intermediate sodium. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.3, p. 7.1-80

The elevation difference between the IHX and the higher AHX drives natural circulation flow. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.3, p. 7.1-80

IHXIHT above PHTIntermediate into primary PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.3.3.3, p. 7.1-63
SHXIHT below NSSSalt into intermediate PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.3, p. 7.1-81

Objective 4SY-04-K4

In an SHX leak, which way does the coolant flow?

The answer is A. IHT pressure is lower than NSS, so NSS coolant flows to the intermediate loop. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.3, p. 7.1-81

Objective 4SY-04-K5

What drives natural circulation in the IHT?

The answer is C. The elevation difference provides the potential energy. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.3, p. 7.1-80

Summary

  • The IHT has two loops, each with an ISP, SHXs, and connections to one IHX and one AHX. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.1, p. 7.1-75
  • IHT pressure sits above primary and below salt. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.3, p. 7.1-81
  • ISP trips are on low expansion tank level, and high high primary sodium level or temperature. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.1.4.3, p. 7.1-81

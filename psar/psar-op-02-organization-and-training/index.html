This lesson covers who runs the plant, who reports to whom, and the rules that apply to you as a licensed operator. PSAR Rev 1 (ML25276A288), Sec. 11.1, pp. 11.1-5 to 11.1-13

Terms with a dotted underline are PSAR acronyms. Hover, tap or press Enter on one to see its meaning.

Learning objectives

1 Describe the operating organization and who is responsible for it.

Taught in: Operating organization

2 State the responsibilities of the managers who report to the Plant General Manager.

Taught in: Plant General Manager and direct reports

3 Explain how independent oversight is arranged.

Taught in: Independent oversight

4 State the qualification and training requirements for operators.

Taught in: Qualification and training

5 Apply the conduct of operations rules for control room activities.

Taught in: Conduct of operations

Operating organization

Objectives 1

USO is responsible for the safe operation of Kemmerer Unit 1. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.4, p. 11.1-6

It establishes an Operating Organization before pre-operational testing. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.4, p. 11.1-6

The PGM and Director of Operations are responsible for the Operating Organization. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.4, p. 11.1-6

  • Engineering
  • Quality assurance, audit, and surveillance
  • Plant sciences (chemistry and radiation protection)
  • Commissioning support
  • Training
  • Maintenance
  • Operations
  • Outside contractual support

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.4, p. 11.1-5

Independent oversight

Objectives 3

The Director of Nuclear Oversight reports to the CNO and manages audits, quality control, and employee concerns. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.4.2, p. 11.1-7

The QA Manager reports to that director and supervises QC inspections, NDE, and QA audits. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.4.2.1, p. 11.1-7

Qualification and training

Objectives 4

Qualifications meet ANSI/ANS-3.1-2014, as endorsed by RG 1.8. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.1, p. 11.1-8

The operator training and requalification plan follows 10 CFR 55 and 10 CFR 50.120. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.2, p. 11.1-8

Qualification includes a comprehensive written exam and an operating test, as required by 10 CFR 55. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.2, p. 11.1-8

Training programs meeting 10 CFR 50.120 will be in place within 18 months prior to fuel load. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.2, p. 11.1-8

The requalification plan will be provided at the OLA stage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.2, p. 11.1-8

Conduct of operations

Objectives 5

Site operations are controlled through the MCR, with some activities coordinated from the online/outage control center. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.3, p. 11.1-8

Maintenance, surveillances, and removal or restoration of safety-significant SSCs may not start without Control Room Supervisor approval. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.3, p. 11.1-8

Rules of practice incorporate RG 1.114. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.3, p. 11.1-9

Reactivity controls are only manipulated by licensed operators and senior operators, except as allowed for training. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.3, p. 11.1-9

Other apparatus that may affect reactivity or power needs the knowledge and consent of the operator or Control Room Supervisor at the controls. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.3, p. 11.1-9

Operators and technicians are expected to follow approved procedures and technical specifications. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.3, p. 11.1-9

Staffing requirements in 10 CFR 50.54(k) and (m) will be given at the OL stage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.3, p. 11.1-9

Knowledge check: Conduct of operations

Objective 5OP-02-K10

Who approves the start of a surveillance?

The answer is D. They may not commence without Control Room Supervisor approval. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.3, p. 11.1-8

Report a problem
Objective 5OP-02-K11

A trainee wants to move a rod under instruction. What allows it?

The answer is B. Only licensed staff manipulate reactivity controls, except as allowed for training under 10 CFR 55. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.3, p. 11.1-9

Report a problem
Objective 5OP-02-K12

Equipment other than controls may affect power. What is needed to operate it?

The answer is A. 10 CFR 50.54 requires knowledge and consent. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.3, p. 11.1-9

Summary

  • USO owns safe operation. The PGM and Director of Operations answer for the Operating Organization. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.4, p. 11.1-6
  • Nuclear Oversight reports to the CNO, independent of the PGM line. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.4.2, p. 11.1-7
  • The Control Room Supervisor approves work on safety-significant SSCs, and only licensed staff move reactivity controls. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.1.5.3, p. 11.1-8

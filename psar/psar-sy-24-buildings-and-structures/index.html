Four buildings make up the Nuclear Island. For each one, learn what it houses and how the part below grade and the part above grade are classified. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.8, pp. 7.8-1 to 7.8-37

Terms with a dotted underline are PSAR acronyms. Hover, tap or press Enter on one to see its meaning.

Learning objectives

1 State the purpose of the Nuclear Island buildings.

Taught in: Overview

2 Identify the Nuclear Island buildings boundaries and interfaces.

Taught in: Overview

3 Identify the major components of the Nuclear Island buildings.

Taught in: Reactor Building, Fuel Handling Building, Reactor Auxiliary Building, Nuclear Island Control Building

4 Explain Nuclear Island buildings operation.

Taught in: Reactor Building

5 Describe the Nuclear Island buildings controls and indications.

Not in PSAR Rev 1. This lesson does not teach it.

6 Explain the Nuclear Island buildings automatic features and interlocks.

Taught in: RXB functions

7 Apply operating procedures for the Nuclear Island buildings.

Not in PSAR Rev 1. This lesson does not teach it.

8 Diagnose abnormal conditions in the Nuclear Island buildings.

Not in PSAR Rev 1. This lesson does not teach it.

9 Explain the safety significance of the Nuclear Island buildings.

Taught in: RXB functions, Safety significance

10 Address technical specifications and administrative controls for the Nuclear Island buildings.

Taught in: Safety significance

Reactor Building

Objectives 3, 4

The SR substructure houses the reactor, RES, and the RAC duct stacks. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.1, p. 7.8-1

The RXB is normally unoccupied, with substructure access restricted during normal operation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.1, p. 7.8-1

Seismic isolators under the RES and MIRSS sit in a subgrade space. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.1.1.1, p. 7.8-1

The HAA contains the reactor head, penetrations, PSPs, and control rod drive assemblies. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.1.1.1, p. 7.8-1

Removable planks in the grade slab give access for maintenance and refueling. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.1.1.1, p. 7.8-2

The HAA is a functional containment boundary with a radionuclide retention function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.1.1.2, p. 7.8-3

The superstructure supports a single-failure proof overhead bridge crane. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.1.2, p. 7.8-7

Fuel Handling Building

Objectives 3

The FHB houses fuel receipt, refueling, storage, radwaste systems, and the SFP. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.2, p. 7.8-10

A three foot thick grade slab protects sub-grade areas and supports the Ex-Vessel Handling Machine and Bottom Loading Transfer Cask. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.2.1.1, p. 7.8-10

The SFP has 5 foot thick walls and a 4 foot thick foundation slab about 51 feet below grade. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.2.1.1, p. 7.8-10

A stainless steel liner and leak chase monitor leakage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.2.1.1, p. 7.8-10

The superstructure is an enveloping barrier supporting negative pressure for radionuclide retention. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.2.2.2, p. 7.8-15

The superstructure is NSRST, seismic class SCN1. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.2.2.2, p. 7.8-16

Reactor Auxiliary Building

Objectives 3

The RAB takes intermediate sodium from the RXB and transfers heat to the NI Salt System. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.3, p. 7.8-20

It houses IHT loops, Sodium-Salt Heat Exchangers, ISPs, IHT expansion tanks, SCG, and SPS equipment. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.3, p. 7.8-20

IAC Air Stack Structures and Equipment sit next to the RAB. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.3, p. 7.8-20

The lower level holds the IHT supplemental tank, 62 feet below grade, and has no personnel access from adjacent spaces. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.3.1.1, p. 7.8-20

SPS Cells are designed for slight negative pressure as part of an enveloping boundary. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.3.1.3, p. 7.8-22

An automobile tornado missile barrier protects the IAC and IHT. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.3.2.3, p. 7.8-25

Roof panels at the east end can be removed for crane access to ISPs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.3.2.1, p. 7.8-24

Nuclear Island Control Building

Objectives 3

The SR substructure houses the RPS, PSP and ISP breakers, NSRST electrical equipment, and the RSC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.4, p. 7.8-29

The NSRST superstructure holds the MCR, and no SR equipment. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.4, p. 7.8-29

Substructure rooms hold NUP and NDC equipment and batteries. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.4.1.1, p. 7.8-29

The superstructure is a one-story steel frame serving as the NI control center. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.4.2.1, p. 7.8-32

A reinforced MCR enclosure protects against chemical, radiological, and weather-related external events. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.4.2.1, p. 7.8-32

The superstructure is designed not to collapse under seismic or tornado loading. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.4.2.3, p. 7.8-34

Safety significance

Objectives 9, 10

Structures provide load paths and external hazard protection for the SSCs inside. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.1.1.2, p. 7.8-5

RAB substructure and superstructure are seismic class SCN1, and so is the NCB superstructure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.4.2.2, p. 7.8-34

Summary

  • RXB, FHB, and NCB have SR substructures, and the RAB is NSRST throughout. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.3, p. 7.8-20
  • The HAA is part of the RXB substructure and is a functional containment boundary. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.1.1.2, p. 7.8-3
  • The MCR is in the NSRST NCB superstructure, and the RPS and RSC are in the SR NCB substructure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.4, p. 7.8-29

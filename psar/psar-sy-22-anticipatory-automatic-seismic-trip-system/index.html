Purpose and interfaces

Objectives 1, 2

The AST measures ground motion, applies processing and logic, and sends actuation signals to the RTBs through the RPS interface device. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1, p. 7.6-75

It is used in seismic beyond design basis events greater than the SSE, to scram before the peak wave reaches the facility. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1, p. 7.6-75

The RTB interface device is downstream of the RPS logic, where an analog relay matrix isolates the RTB power supply control. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1, p. 7.6-75

Components and locations

Objectives 3

  • Triaxial seismic accelerometers
  • Signal processing unit
  • Bi-stable unit

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1.1, p. 7.6-75

There are four channels. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1.1, p. 7.6-75

Accelerometers are in boreholes at four locations at 90-degree angles around the RXB. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1.2, p. 7.6-75

Signal processing is in cabinets in the NCB substructure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1.2, p. 7.6-75

Specific cabinet locations will be given at the OL stage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1.2, p. 7.6-75

Operation and logic

Objectives 4, 6

If the processing output passes the bi-stable setpoint, the bi-stable outputs an isolated signal to the shunt trip feature in the RTB. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1.1, p. 7.6-75

The RTB arrangement gives 2-out-of-4 logic for reactor scram. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1.1, p. 7.6-75

One failed channel cannot cause a spurious trip, because two RTBs on different channels are needed. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1.1, p. 7.6-75

Its function is DL4-RC4, automatic seismic trip. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.2.1, p. 7.6-77

Testing and safety significance

Objectives 8, 9, 10

The AST is NSRST. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.2, p. 7.6-75

Its components are seismic class SCN1. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.3, p. 7.6-78

It conforms to IEEE 338-2022 for testing and calibration. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.3.2, p. 7.6-78

IEEE 379-2014 addresses single failure and IEEE 384-2018 addresses independence. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.3.3, p. 7.6-78

Summary

  • Four accelerometer channels in boreholes around the RXB feed bi-stables that use the RTB shunt trip. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1.1, p. 7.6-75
  • The RTB arrangement gives 2-out-of-4 logic, so one bad channel cannot trip the plant. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.1.1, p. 7.6-75
  • The AST is NSRST function DL4-RC4 and acts for seismic beyond design basis events. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.8.2.1, p. 7.6-77

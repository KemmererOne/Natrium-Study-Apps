Purpose and layout

Objectives 1, 2, 3

The CRD has 13 identical control rod drives: 9 for the primary control rod assemblies and 4 for the secondary. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

The CRD supports normal reactivity control and reactor scram. Portions of it provide a primary coolant boundary function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

  • CRDM and housing
  • Motor drive assembly
  • Motor controller
  • Driveline assembly
  • Scram valve and cylinder assembly
  • Seals

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

All of the CRD except the motor controller is in the HAA of the RXB substructure. The motor controller is in the NCB substructure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

The CRDM housings are welded to and supported by penetrations in the RPA. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

Latching the rod

Objectives 3, 4

The driveline is directly coupled to the CRA through a pneumatically controlled latching mechanism in normal operation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

The latch engages when the scram valve and cylinder assembly is pressurized with inert gas from the SCG. It releases when gas pressure vents. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

The assembly has three electric solenoid-operated pilot valves that supply gas to a piston and cylinder. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

The scram valves are fail-safe, de-energize to vent, and are controlled by the RPS. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

Normal rod motion

Objectives 4, 5

The CRD positions CRAs for start-up, power control, power shaping, and shutdown from RMC demand signals. The RMC is a subsystem of the NIC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

Independent drives allow individual CRA positioning, and a fault in one drive does not affect the others. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

A ball screw drive assembly converts motor rotation to driveline linear motion. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

An electro-mechanical brake prevents driveline motion unless a demand signal is present. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

High speed motor-driven insertion provides power run-back capability. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

The motor controller and drive are powered from the Nuclear Island Uninterruptible AC Power Supply System. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-58

Scram

Objectives 4, 6

Scram starts on two out of four scram signals from the RPS. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.3, p. 7.2-65

The RPS signal de-energizes the scram SOVs, which vent the cylinder and disengage the CRA. The CRA inserts by gravity. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

Two of the three scram valves are enough to vent the cylinder, so failure of one valve does not prevent scram or cause a spurious scram. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.2, p. 7.2-62

The scram valves also vent on loss of power to the SOV. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.3, p. 7.2-65

The scram valve and cylinder assembly is inside the CRDM housing and is not exposed to primary sodium. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.3, p. 7.2-65

The RPS signal also starts driveline scram follow, where the motor drives the driveline down at maximum insertion speed. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

Scram follow gives assurance that the CRA is fully inserted and provides insertion position indication. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

Knowledge check: Scram

Objective 4,6SY-09-K7

How many of the three scram valves must actuate to release a CRA?

The answer is B. Two of three is sufficient, so a single valve failure does not prevent scram. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.3, p. 7.2-65

Report a problem
Objective 4,6SY-09-K8

What inserts the CRA in the SR scram function?

The answer is A. The released CRA inserts into the core by gravity. Motor scram follow is NSRST. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

Report a problem
Objective 4,6SY-09-K9

Power is lost to the scram SOVs. What happens?

The answer is D. Scram valves vent the scram cylinder on loss of power to the SOV. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.3, p. 7.2-65

Report a problem
Objective 4,6SY-09-K10

What is the purpose of driveline scram follow?

The answer is B. Scram follow provides assurance of full insertion and position indication. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

Position indication and instruments

Objectives 5, 8

Coupled CRA position goes to the RMC through motor shaft and ball screw revolutions monitoring and a linear position transducer. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

CRA scram insertion is indicated by full driveline scram follow insertion position. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

The CRDM also has scram valve position indication and thermocouples that interface with the NIC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.5, p. 7.2-65

Housing, seals, and purge

Objectives 2, 3, 8

The CRDM housing is a lower housing flanged to an upper housing, with a top access plate. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

Metallic seals and bellows keep cover gas and aerosolized sodium from entering the lower housing. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

The housing runs with an inert gas purge and static overpressure from the SCG, so leakage flows into the cover gas space. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

Purge gas flow monitors in the SCG supply line identify leakage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59

The CRDMs are cooled by the ambient environment of the HAA. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.3, p. 7.2-64

Summary

  • The 13 drives use a gas-held latch. Venting the cylinder releases the CRA to insert by gravity. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.1, p. 7.2-59
  • Two of three fail-safe scram valves are enough. A scram starts on two of four RPS signals. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.5.3, p. 7.2-65

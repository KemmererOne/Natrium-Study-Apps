Purpose and classification

Objectives 1, 2, 9

The RWG processes gaseous waste from the primary SCG and the SCG exhaust distribution cabinet. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.1, p. 7.4-1

It gives holdup for decay of short-lived isotopes such as argon-41. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.1, p. 7.4-1

Charcoal beds give enhanced decay of longer lived isotopes like xenon and krypton before stack discharge. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.1, p. 7.4-1

The discharge is diluted by NHV airflow, and the combined exhaust is monitored and recorded. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.1, p. 7.4-1

The RWG is NSRST because it supports the retain radionuclides fundamental safety function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.1, p. 7.4-1

Reactor operation and safe shutdown do not require the RWG. Its failure does not compromise any SR system or component. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.1, p. 7.4-1

Liquid radwaste and solid radwaste are NST systems described in Secs. 9.1 and 9.3. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4, p. 7.4-1

Flow path

Objectives 3, 4

The RWG is below grade in the FHB. It is a once-through, ambient temperature charcoal adsorber delay system. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-4

StepComponentJob
1Vacuum tankReceives waste gas and gives short-term vacuum if compressor service is interrupted PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-4
2Particulate filterUpstream of each compressor PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5
3CompressorOne of two in parallel makes the vacuum PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-4
4AftercoolerChilled water from the NI Water System cools the gas PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5
5Holdup tankDelays flow 24 hours for argon-41 decay PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5
6Pressure reducing valvePrevents high pressure in the charcoal beds PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5
7Charcoal adsorber bedsFour in parallel, optimized for xenon and krypton retention PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5
8NHV plant stackMonitored effluent path to the environment PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-6

The holdup tank is baffled to minimize gas mixing and maximize retention time. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5

HEPA filters are upstream and downstream of each charcoal bed. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5

Knowledge check: Flow path

Objective 3,4SY-13-K3

How long does the holdup tank delay flow?

The answer is C. The tank delays flow for 24 hours to allow decay of argon-41. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5

Objective 3,4SY-13-K4

What do the charcoal beds retain?

The answer is B. The charcoal is optimized for xenon and krypton retention. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5

Objective 3,4SY-13-K5

How are the charcoal beds arranged?

The answer is B. There are four beds in parallel. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5

Automatic features

Objectives 5, 6, 8

A single compressor starts when vacuum pressure reaches a setpoint. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5

On compressor failure, the failed compressor isolates and the alternate starts. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5

A high aftercooler outlet temperature alarms to alert the operator of inadequate cooling. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5

A filter with high differential pressure from particulate buildup can be isolated and replaced. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5

Radiation monitors on each charcoal bed discharge isolate flow on high radiation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5

Radioactivity readings give automatic termination of releases as necessary. Valves for this are designed to fail closed. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.5, p. 7.4-7

Radiation readouts are in the MCR, with more readouts in controlled areas. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.5, p. 7.4-7

The Auxiliary Monitoring and Control System sends control signals and receives flow, pressure, and temperature data. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.5, p. 7.4-7

Modes and power

Objectives 4, 9

The RWG is operable in all modes of reactor operation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.2, p. 7.4-6

It is not required to mitigate design basis events or accidents. It operates during normal operations and AOOs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.2, p. 7.4-6

It does not need power for any NSRST function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-6

Safety significance

Objectives 9, 10

DL2-RR4 is the RWG function: RWG components, including seals, form a radionuclide confinement boundary that prevents uncontrolled release. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.2, p. 7.4-3

The design conforms to RG 8.8 with shielded cubicles and remotely operated equipment for ALARA. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.2, p. 7.4-4

RWG conformance to RG 1.143 is partial. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.2, p. 7.4-4

A cost-benefit analysis will be done at the OL stage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.2, p. 7.4-3

Summary

  • Waste gas goes through a vacuum tank, compressor, 24 hour holdup tank, and four charcoal beds to the monitored stack. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-5
  • High radiation isolates charcoal bed flow, and release termination valves fail closed. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.5, p. 7.4-7
  • The RWG is NSRST, is not needed for safe shutdown, and needs no power for its NSRST function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.4.1.3.1, p. 7.4-6

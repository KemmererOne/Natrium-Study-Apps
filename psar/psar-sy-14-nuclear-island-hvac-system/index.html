The NHV is planned building by building. Its safety-significant work is isolating penetrations, filtering exhaust, and keeping the control room habitable. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.5.1, pp. 7.5-1 to 7.5-9

Purpose and layout

Objectives 1, 2

The NHV is allocated per building. It provides fresh air and controls exhaust from all NI buildings. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.1, p. 7.5-1

The RAB, RXB, FHB, and FAB have RCAs, where airflow goes from lesser to higher potential contamination. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.1, p. 7.5-1

The NCB does not contain RCAs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.1, p. 7.5-1

The NHV is NSRST to support radionuclide retention and MCR isolation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.1, p. 7.5-1

The controlled combined release path from the FHB, RXB, RAB, and FAB through the monitored plant stack is an NSRST function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-6

Reactor Building

Objectives 3, 4, 6

RXB HVAC keeps the building at slightly negative pressure with NST HEPA exhaust units. Exhaust goes to the monitored stack. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-5

HAA isolation uses one inboard and one outboard air operated valve pair for each penetration. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-5

The below grade SCG vapor trap cell is slightly negative with an inerted atmosphere and is monitored for SCG leakage. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-5

The reactor overpressure protection release point is the SCG trap cell. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-5

The trap cell is automatically isolated on leakage indication in the MCR. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-5

The NHV cooling loop isolates automatically on reactor vessel relief valve position. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-5

Reactor Auxiliary Building

Objectives 3, 4, 6

RAB HVAC also keeps the building slightly negative, using NST HEPA exhaust units to the monitored stack. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-5

Below grade SPS cells are slightly negative with inerted atmosphere during operations. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-5

SPS cell leak detectors connect to an NFP sodium leak detection control panel. It sends signals to the utility monitoring and control system for cell isolation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-6

Fuel Handling Building

Objectives 3, 4, 8

FHB normal exhaust goes through two 50 percent HEPA filtration units. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-6

Differential pressure monitors on the main working floor control exhaust flow to keep the FHB slightly negative. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-6

All FHB exhaust goes through welded ductwork, HEPA filters, and the monitored stack. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-6

High radiation on HEPA filter discharge alarms in the MCR for manual operator action. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-6

Control room habitability

Objectives 4, 5, 8

Sodium monitors, toxic chemical sensors, and a radiation monitor continually sample the MCR fresh air intake. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-6

On an elevated hazard, an MCR alarm alerts operators to place MCR HVAC units in recirculation and deenergize the below grade HVAC systems. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-6

Deenergizing below grade HVAC prevents similar hazards in the remote shutdown facility. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-6

Recirculation credits NSRST HEPA filters to minimize operator dose. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-6

If MCR personnel evacuate to the RSC, a manually operated compressed breathable air bottle rack supports post accident monitoring. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.2, p. 7.5-4

Knowledge check: Automatic versus manual isolation

Objective 5,6,8SY-14-K9

The RV pressure relief valve opens. What happens to the SCG trap cell?

The answer is A. The control system monitors valve position and automatically isolates the trap cell. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.5, p. 7.5-7

Objective 5,6,8SY-14-K10

High radiation is indicated in the SPS cells HVAC. What is the response?

The answer is C. Operations personnel evaluate the condition and initiate a manual isolation, as necessary. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.5, p. 7.5-7

Objective 5,6,8SY-14-K11

Power is lost to NHV isolation valves. What is their position?

The answer is A. Valves and dampers are designed to fail closed. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-5

Summary

  • Buildings with RCAs run slightly negative, with airflow toward higher potential contamination and exhaust through the monitored stack. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.1, p. 7.5-1
  • Leak signals and relief valve opening isolate cells automatically. High radiation indications lead to manual isolation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.5, p. 7.5-7
  • Isolation valves and dampers fail closed. Operators protect the MCR by placing HVAC in recirculation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.1.3, p. 7.5-5

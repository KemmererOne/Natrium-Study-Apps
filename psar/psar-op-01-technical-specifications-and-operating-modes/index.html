Knowledge check: Operating modes

Objective 1OP-01-K1

What separates Mode 1 from Mode 2?

The answer is A. Mode 1 cools through the NSS, and Mode 2 through the IAC. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-10

Report a problem
Objective 1OP-01-K2

What separates Mode 3 from Mode 4?

The answer is A. Mode 3 is above 400 F, and Mode 4 is at or below it. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-10

Report a problem
Objective 1OP-01-K3

The plant is at 450 F with one CRA coupled. What mode?

The answer is A. No more than one CRA coupled and above 400 F is Mode 3. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-10

Report a problem
Objective 1OP-01-K4

Which modes have more than one CRA coupled?

The answer is A. Modes 1 and 2 have more than one CRA coupled. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-10

Mode descriptions and transitions

Objectives 2

Mode 1 is entered from Mode 2 during startup when heat removal transfers from the IAC to the NSS through the SHXs. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.1, p. 11.5-1

Mode 2 is entered in startup when the reactor scram breakers are closed and more than one CRA is coupled. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.2, p. 11.5-1

Mode 2 supports rod withdrawal to critical and power ascension within IAC heat removal capability. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.2, p. 11.5-1

Mode 2 is not required during a shutdown. A scram before Mode 2 conditions lets the plant go directly from Mode 1 to Mode 3. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.2, p. 11.5-2

Mode 3 is a transition state. The primary coolant boundary is established during startup in Mode 3. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.3, p. 11.5-2

Mode 3 supports plant heatup through PSP operation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.3, p. 11.5-2

In Modes 3 and 4, one CRA may be coupled and manipulated for testing. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.3, p. 11.5-2

In Mode 4, hot pool temperature is lowered below 400 F (204 C). PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-2

TransitionMarker
Mode 2 to Mode 1IAC blower off, and top and bottom dampers closed and latched PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-10
Mode 1 to Mode 2NSS isolation valves closed PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-10

Why is no more than one CRA coupled in Modes 3 and 4?

Safety Limits

Objectives 4

Safety limits protect the integrity of barriers that guard against uncontrolled release of radioactivity. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-3

LSSSs are settings for automatic protective devices. A setting must make protective action correct the situation before a Safety Limit is exceeded. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-3

Probable SL subjects are fuel cladding temperature and peak fuel temperature. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-3

Cladding temperature limits protect against thermal creep and wall thinning from fuel-cladding chemical and eutectic interactions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-3

Peak fuel temperature limits protect against melting. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-3

Cycle-specific limits are in the Core Operating Limits Report (COLR). PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-3

A violation gives direction to restore compliance and shut down the reactor. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-4

A setpoint control program, consistent with Option B of TSTF-493, replaces explicit LSSS values. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-3

Limiting Conditions for Operation

Objectives 5

LCOs are the lowest functional capability or performance levels of equipment required for safe operation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-4

Surveillance requirements cover test, calibration, or inspection. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 11.5.2.4, p. 11.5-4

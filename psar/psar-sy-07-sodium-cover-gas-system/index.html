Purpose and scope

Objectives 1, 2

The SCG controls, monitors, and supplies inert argon gas in the RXB, RAB, and FHB. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.1, p. 7.2-22

It comprises the Primary SCG and the Intermediate SCG. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.1, p. 7.2-22

Both receive purified argon from the NGA, with design conditions of 250 psig and 110 F. Local accumulators add inventory if NGA supply is disrupted. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.1.1, p. 7.2-22

SCG piping that penetrates the RES, up to and including the first isolation valve, is part of the primary coolant boundary. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.2.2, p. 7.2-31

Primary SCG flow path

Objectives 3, 4

All gas entering the RV exhausts through a single port in normal operation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.1.1, p. 7.2-22

In the primary filter skid, gas goes through the vapor trap (a preheater and vapor condenser), then an aerosol filter to remove sodium vapors and aerosols. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.1.1, p. 7.2-22

If cesium is present, gas goes through a cesium vapor filter before exhausting to the RWG. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.1.1, p. 7.2-22

RV-GV annulus gas is generally recycled in a closed loop and exhausts only to relieve high pressure, to RWG or NHV depending on activity. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.1.1, p. 7.2-22

The highest delivered pressure is to the scram cylinders, which sets the minimum NGA delivered pressure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.1.1, p. 7.2-22

Pressure relationships

Objectives 4

The RV cover gas stays at a slight positive pressure relative to the HAA. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.3, p. 7.2-33

GV pressure is kept above HAA pressure and below reactor pressure. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.3, p. 7.2-34

A minimum flow in the main feed line prevents sodium and radioisotope diffusion that could impact overpressure protection. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.2.1, p. 7.2-28

Heat tracing keeps sodium in the exhaust from freezing and plugging when the condenser and filters are unavailable. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.2.1, p. 7.2-28

Failed fuel monitoring

Objectives 5, 8

  • Impurity monitoring for hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, and methane
  • Gas sampling
  • Gamma spectroscopy and radiation monitoring for failed fuel
  • Tag gas monitoring for failed fuel localization

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.3, p. 7.2-34

The exhaust stream is monitored for fission products. With no fission gas, it goes directly to the RWG vacuum tank. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.3, p. 7.2-34

If an increase in fission gas is detected, the stream goes to the tag gas analysis cabinet, where xenon and krypton are concentrated. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.3, p. 7.2-34

Overpressure protection

Objectives 6

On overpressure, SCG relief valves open and discharge to a holdup volume. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.2.1, p. 7.2-27

The vapor trap cell is isolated on a primary coolant overpressure relief valve open indication. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.2.2, p. 7.2-31

DL3-RR7 covers RV internal overpressure and RV external overpressure, meaning the RV-GV annulus and CRDM housings. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.3.2.2, p. 7.2-30

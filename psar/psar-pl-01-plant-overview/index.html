What Kemmerer Unit 1 is

Objectives 1

Kemmerer Unit 1 is a Natrium reactor plant in Lincoln County, Wyoming, about 3 miles southwest of Kemmerer. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1, p. 1.1-1

It is licensed under 10 CFR Part 50, and this PSAR supports the construction permit application. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1, p. 1.1-1

"The Natrium reactor plant is an 840 MWt pool-type sodium fast reactor (SFR) that contains a compact and simple safety envelope and a molten salt energy storage system which enables the plant to vary its supply of energy to the grid, up to 500 megawatts electric (MWe) net, while maintaining constant reactor power." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-1

The plant is built as part of the DOE Advanced Reactor Demonstration Program. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.2, p. 1.1-1

The reactor uses sodium-bonded metallic fuel with HT9 cladding, which is a high chromium martensitic steel. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1, p. 1.1-2

Knowledge check: What Kemmerer Unit 1 is

Objective 1PL-01-K1

What is the licensed thermal rating described for Kemmerer Unit 1?

The answer is B. The PSAR describes an 840 MWt reactor. The 500 figure is net electrical output in megawatts electric, available through the salt storage system. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-1

Report a problem
Objective 1PL-01-K2

What lets the plant vary its supply to the grid while reactor power stays constant?

The answer is C. The molten salt storage system lets grid output vary up to 500 MWe net while reactor power stays constant. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-1

The heat path

Objectives 2

The reactor operates near atmospheric pressure, and pumps circulate sodium through the core. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-1

StepFromToThrough
1CorePrimary sodium (PHT)Upward flow through the core assemblies PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1.1, p. 1.1-3
2Primary sodium (PHT)Intermediate sodium (IHT)Intermediate heat exchangers in the reactor pool PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-1
3Intermediate sodium (IHT)Nuclear Island Salt System (NSS)Sodium-salt heat exchangers PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-1
4NSSHot salt storage tank (EI)Salt piping between the islands PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-1
5Hot saltSteam turbine generatorsSteam generation PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-2

During startup and shutdown the intermediate loop rejects heat to the atmosphere through the Intermediate Air Cooling System sodium-air heat exchanger instead of the salt path. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1, p. 1.1-3

Knowledge check: The heat path

Objective 2PL-01-K3

Where does heat cross from primary sodium to intermediate sodium?

The answer is A. Heat moves from the primary loop to the intermediate loop through the intermediate heat exchangers in the reactor pool. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-1

Report a problem
Objective 2PL-01-K4

Where does the intermediate loop send heat during startup and shutdown?

The answer is A. The intermediate loop transfers heat to the NSS for normal steady operation, and to the atmosphere through the IAC during startup and shutdown. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1, p. 1.1-3

Nuclear Island and Energy Island

Objectives 3

The Nuclear Island contains the reactor and its supporting systems. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-2

The Energy Island contains the thermal energy storage tanks, steam generator, feedwater system, condenser, turbine, and supporting systems. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-2

"The EI structures, systems, and components (SSCs) are classified as non-safety-related with no special treatment (NST)." PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-2

The salt system physically connects the islands and stores excess thermal energy, so steam generation can run independently from reactor power operations. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-2

Inherent and passive features

Objectives 4

The PSAR names near-atmospheric operating pressures, always-on passive air cooling, and inherent reactivity feedback as reliable inherent and passive safety features. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-2

Safety functions are made integral to the reactor vessel, and support equipment moves to separate structures, which simplifies the reactor building. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1, p. 1.1-2

Reactor systems at a glance

Objectives 5

SystemFunction in one line
Reactor Core System (RCC)Sustains the chain reaction for fission heat within thermal-mechanical limits PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1.1, p. 1.1-3
Reactor Enclosure System (RES)Supports the PHT, RCC, and CRD, and contains the primary sodium PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1.2, p. 1.1-3
Primary Heat Transport (PHT)Moves heat from the core to the IHT PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1.4, p. 1.1-4
Intermediate Heat Transport (IHT)Moves heat away from the PHT to the NSS or the IAC PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1.5, p. 1.1-4
Reactor Air Cooling (RAC)Passive decay heat removal by natural convection of air PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1.3, p. 1.1-4
Intermediate Air Cooling (IAC)Preferred heat removal system during upset events PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1.6, p. 1.1-5

Knowledge check: Reactor systems at a glance

Objective 5PL-01-K7

Which system does the PSAR call the preferred heat removal system during upset events?

The answer is C. The IAC is the preferred heat removal system during upset events. The RAC is the always-on passive system. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1.6, p. 1.1-5

Report a problem
Objective 5PL-01-K8

What is the primary objective of the RAC?

The answer is A. RAC provides passive decay heat removal to the environment through natural convection of air. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.1.3, p. 1.1-4

Summary

  • Kemmerer Unit 1 is an 840 MWt pool-type sodium fast reactor with molten salt energy storage and up to 500 MWe net to the grid. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-1
  • Heat flows from core to primary sodium, to intermediate sodium, to salt, and then to steam. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-2
  • The Energy Island is non-safety-related with no special treatment. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.3, p. 1.1-2

RAC is the always-on passive heat removal path. Air rises past the guard vessel and leaves through tall stacks. There is nothing to start and nothing to stop. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.2.1, pp. 7.2-1 to 7.2-12

Purpose

Objectives 1

RAC supports passive reactor decay heat removal by natural convection air flow and heat rejection to the atmosphere. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-1

With the PHT and RES, it provides the long-term emergency core cooling function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-1

RAC is continuously in operation and requires no actuations or operator action to perform its heat removal safety function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-1

Components and boundaries

Objectives 2, 3

  • Four inlet stacks and four outlet stacks
  • Collector Cylinder Assembly (CCA)
  • Inlet and outlet ducting

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-1

RAC is integrated into the RXB substructure. Two inlet and two outlet stacks stand on each of the north and south sides. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-1

Stack outlets are higher than the inlets, which limits recirculation of air. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-1

Inlet stacks terminate at least 30 feet above grade. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-1

Fixed louvers protect the inlets, and chimney cowls protect the outlets. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-1

Operation

Objectives 4

The riser annulus is formed by the collector cylinder and the GV. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-2

Heated air in the riser rises, drawing ambient air through the inlets and down the downcomer annulus. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-2

The collector cylinder is heated by thermal radiation from the GV outer surface. Air is heated by convection from the GV and collector cylinder. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-2

Self-regulating capacity

Objectives 4, 8

Without heat removal to the IHT, sodium near the RV wall gets hotter, the GV gets hotter, radiation increases, and air flow and temperature rise. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.3, p. 7.2-7

RAC heat removal capacity increases or decreases based on the heat removal requirements. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.3, p. 7.2-7

When heat goes from the PHT to the IHT, the GV is cooler and RAC removes less heat. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.3, p. 7.2-7

What happens to RAC heat removal when heat transfer to the IHT is lost?

The answer is B. Higher GV temperature increases radiation and air flow, so heat rejection increases. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.3, p. 7.2-7

Indications

Objectives 5

RAC inlet temperature, outlet temperature, and outlet mass flow rate are monitored. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.5, p. 7.2-7

Each inlet stack has a temperature instrument, and each outlet stack has temperature and mass flow instruments. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.5, p. 7.2-7

Signal output goes directly to the RPS, with redundant instruments on a separate division. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.5, p. 7.2-7

The parameters are IEEE 497-2016 variable Types B and D for PAM. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.5, p. 7.2-7

Degradation and hazards

Objectives 8

Redundant stacks with common plenums compensate for potential blockage of a stack opening. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.3, p. 7.2-6

Continuous operation and monitoring give indication of performance degradation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.3, p. 7.2-6

The inlet louvers have provisions for temporary fine screens in severe small debris or dust conditions. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-1

The design includes features for remote vacuuming of the internal air flow path during outages. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.3, p. 7.2-6

RAC air activation produces N-16 and Ar-41 gaseous effluents, and outlet flow is monitored to quantify discharge. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.2, p. 7.2-4

Summary

  • RAC is passive, continuous, and needs no operator action. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.1, p. 7.2-1
  • Its heat removal rises and falls with GV temperature. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.3, p. 7.2-7
  • Inlet and outlet temperature and outlet mass flow show its performance. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.1.5, p. 7.2-7

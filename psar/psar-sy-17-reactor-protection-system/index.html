The RPS is the safety-related logic that scrams the reactor and trips pumps. Four divisions, two-out-of-four voting, and de-energize to trip are the three ideas to master. PSAR Rev 1 (NAT-9400), Sec. 7.6.3, pp. 7.6-18 to 7.6-35

Terms with a dotted underline are PSAR acronyms. Hover, tap or press Enter on one to see its meaning.

Purpose and divisions

Objectives 1, 2, 3

The RPS takes plant instrument signals, applies logic, and generates outputs for reactor scram and ESF actuations. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1, p. 7.6-18

It is arranged into four redundant divisions, A, B, C, and D. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1, p. 7.6-18

Each process variable is measured by four redundant instruments or groups of instruments. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1, p. 7.6-18

RPS equipment is in four division-specific vaults, DCS, and CRD cabinet rooms in the NCB substructure, and in the MCR. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1.2, p. 7.6-19

The vault and cabinet rooms contain RPS electronics cabinets and reactor trip breakers (RTBs). PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1.2, p. 7.6-19

The RPS uses the RadICS platform. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1.1, p. 7.6-19

Signal path and voting

Objectives 4

StepWhat happens
1Each division receives analog signals from one division of sensors PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1, p. 7.6-18
2The safety function chassis converts signals to digital and applies logic PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1, p. 7.6-18
3Variables are compared to setpoints in that division PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1, p. 7.6-18
4A division above setpoint asserts a divisional vote PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1, p. 7.6-18
5Each division shares its vote with all other divisions PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1, p. 7.6-18
6With two or more votes on the same parameter, a division generates its output PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1, p. 7.6-18

The RPS actuates when at least 2 out of the 4 divisions determine action is required. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2.2, p. 7.6-27

De-energize to trip

Objectives 4, 6, 8

In normal operation, the RPS keeps voltage on the RTB undervoltage coils, which holds RTBs closed. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2.2, p. 7.6-28

Closed RTBs keep power on CRD solenoid valve coils, which keep argon pressure to hold the rods. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2.2, p. 7.6-28

On loss of incoming power, RTBs open, CRD argon vents, and rods drop by gravity. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2.2, p. 7.6-28

ESF circuits also de-energize to trip. On loss of power, relay contacts open and pump breakers trip. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2.2, p. 7.6-28

On a critical fault found by self-diagnostics, the system places all outputs in the safe, tripped state. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2.2, p. 7.6-28

Range-checking detects loss of power at a transmitting system, and the RPS places the associated outputs in the safe state. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2.2, p. 7.6-28

Once started, the sequence of protective actions continues to completion. Deliberate operator action is required to return the RPS to normal. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.3.3, p. 7.6-32

Testing and single failure

Objectives 8, 10

Any division can be out of service for testing at any time without loss of any safety function. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2.2, p. 7.6-27

The RPS withstands a single failure in any operating division, even with one division out for testing. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2.2, p. 7.6-27

Testing and calibration can occur during power operation. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.3.2, p. 7.6-31

  • Cross-checking between redundant channels
  • Introducing and varying substitute input
  • Perturbing the monitored variable

PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.3.2, p. 7.6-31

Setpoints follow ANSI/ISA 67.04.01-2018 as endorsed by RG 1.105. The setpoint control program is in Table 11.5-1. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2, p. 7.6-20

Bypassed and inoperable status indication conforms to RG 1.47. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2, p. 7.6-21

Summary

  • Four divisions vote parameter by parameter, and 2 out of 4 on the same parameter actuates. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2.2, p. 7.6-27
  • Scram and ESF circuits are de-energize to trip, and critical faults go to the safe state. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.2.2, p. 7.6-28
  • A division can be out for test with no loss of function. Manual functions are diverse and not credited. PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.6.3.1, p. 7.6-18

# SY-09 | Control Rod Drive System | system | CRD
range: 7.2-58 7.2-71
sec: Sec. 7.2.5, pp. 7.2-58 to 7.2-71
intro: The CRD moves the control rods and lets them go. The motor gives fine control and fast insertion. The scram itself needs only gas venting and gravity.

== Scram | 4,6
S Scram starts on two out of four scram signals from the RPS. @@ A scram is initiated upon receiving two out of four scram signals from the RPS
S The RPS signal de-energizes the scram SOVs, which vent the cylinder and disengage the CRA. The CRA inserts by gravity. @@ The RPS scram signal results in de-energizing the scram solenoid-operated valves

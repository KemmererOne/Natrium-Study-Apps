== SR, NSRST, and NST | 2
T Class | Meaning
R SR | PRA safety functions credited to keep the consequence of a DBE, or the frequency of a high-consequence BDBE, inside the F-C Target, and to conservatively ensure 10 CFR 50.34 dose requirements can be met @@ credited to maintain the consequence of one or more design basis events
R NSRST | Non-SR PSFs that are risk-significant or required for DID adequacy @@ are the non-SR PSFs that are risk-significant or are required for defense-in-depth
R NST | PSFs that are not SR or NSRST. They are not safety-significant and are not listed in Chapter 5 @@ NST functions are not safety-significant and are not listed in this chapter
S An SSC is risk-significant if its function is needed to keep any LBE inside the F-C Target, or if the total frequency of LBEs with the SSC failed is within one percent of any of the three cumulative risk targets. @@ SSCs are classified as risk-significant if the SSC function is necessary to keep any LBEs inside the F-C Target
S The term SR function is synonymous with required safety function in NEI 18-04. @@ The term SR function is synonymous with the term required safety function
K A non-SR function required for defense-in-depth adequacy is classified as what? | SR | NSRST | NST | DBHL | 1 | NSRST functions are non-SR PSFs that are risk-significant or required for DID adequacy. @@ are the non-SR PSFs that are risk-significant or are required for defense-in-depth

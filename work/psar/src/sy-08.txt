# SY-08 | Sodium Processing System | system | SPS
range: 7.2-41 7.2-57
sec: Sec. 7.2.4, pp. 7.2-41 to 7.2-57
intro: The SPS keeps three sodium inventories clean. Its monitoring and purification jobs are not safety functions, but the piping, pump trip, and leak response around them are.

== What an AOO is | 1
Q Anticipated operational occurrences (AOOs) are anticipated event sequences expected to occur one or more times during the life of the plant.
S Event sequences with mean frequencies of 1x10-2 per plant-year and greater are classified as AOOs. @@ 1x10-2 per plant-year and greater are classified as AOOs
S AOOs take into account the expected responses of all SSCs regardless of safety classification. @@ AOOs take into account the expected
K What frequency classifies an event sequence as an AOO? | 1x10-2 per plant-year and greater | 1x10-4 to 1x10-2 per plant-year | Below 1x10-4 per plant-year | Exactly once per plant life | 0 | Mean frequencies of 1x10-2 per plant-year and greater are classified as AOOs. @@ 1x10-2 per plant-year and greater are classified as AOOs

# SY-03 | Primary Heat Transport System | system | PHT
range: 7.1-50 7.1-74
sec: Sec. 7.1.3, pp. 7.1-50 to 7.1-74
intro: The PHT moves core heat to the intermediate loops. It has two pumps, two heat exchangers, and three sodium pools, and it keeps working by natural circulation when the pumps stop.

== Purpose and boundaries | 1,2
S The primary function of the PHT is to transport heat from the reactor core to the IHT. @@ The primary function of the PHT is to transport heat from the reactor core
S A safety-significant function is to keep primary sodium temperatures within limits that preclude damage to the reactor, fuel, or internals. @@ maintain primary sodium temperatures within design limits
S The PHT includes two IHXs, two PSPs, and three sodium pools inside the RV. PSP flanges, IHX flanges, and PSP motors are above the reactor head. @@ The PSP flanges, IHX flanges, and PSP motors are located above the reactor head

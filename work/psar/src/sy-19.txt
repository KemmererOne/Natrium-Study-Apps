# SY-19 | Reactor Instrumentation System | system | RIS
range: 7.6-48 7.6-59
sec: Sec. 7.6.5, pp. 7.6-48 to 7.6-59
intro: The RIS is the set of non-nuclear sensors in or near the reactor vessel. It measures pool temperature, level, and pump data, and has no displays or logic of its own.

== Purpose and architecture | 1,2,3
S The RIS measures selected parameters in or near the reactor vessel and sends signals to the RPS and NIC. @@ contains instrumentation to measure selected parameters in or near the reactor vessel
S A channel is a single sensor plus its cabling and signal amplification electronics. @@ where a channel is defined as a single sensor and its associated cabling and signal amplification electronics
S The RIS has four redundant, independent divisions for parameters that give SR inputs to the RPS. @@ has four redundant and independent divisions for parameters that provide SR inputs to the RPS

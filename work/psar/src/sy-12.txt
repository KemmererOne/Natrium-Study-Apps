# SY-12 | In-Vessel Fuel Handling System | system | FHI
range: 7.3-40 7.3-53
sec: Sec. 7.3.3, pp. 7.3-40 to 7.3-53
intro: The FHI moves core assemblies inside the reactor vessel during Mode 4. While installed, its machines become part of the functional containment boundary.

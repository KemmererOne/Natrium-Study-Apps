# SY-23 | Nuclear Island Auxiliary Electrical Systems | system | NUP and NDC
range: 7.7-1 7.7-10
sec: Sec. 7.7.1, pp. 7.7-1 to 7.7-10
intro: The NUP and NDC give the Nuclear Island uninterruptible AC and DC power. They are not safety related. The safety functions do not depend on them, which is a key design point.

== Purpose and classification | 1,9
S NUP is the uninterruptible AC system, and NDC is the DC system. Both power NI systems in all modes. @@ The NDC and NUP provide power to NI systems and components during all modes of plant operation
S Failures of the NUP or NDC do not affect the plant's ability to perform SR functions. @@ Failures of the NUP or NDC do not affect the ability of the plant to perform safety-related (SR) functions
S Portions in Divisions A, B, C, and D are NSRST, because they power NSRST SSC functions. @@ Certain portions of the NUP and NDC (comprising Divisions A, B, C, and D) are classified as NSRST
S Electrical systems are otherwise NST. @@ These systems are classified as non-safety-related with no special treatment (NST)

== Automatic transfers and abnormal response | 6,8
S If an inverter fails or there is an output overload or short, the STS automatically transfers UPS loads to the RVT. @@ the inverter’s STS initiates an automatic transfer of the AC UPS loads to the RVT
S For inverter maintenance, loads go to the RVT using the RMBS. @@ the loads can be transferred to the RVT using the RMBS
S On loss of offsite power, NST SDGs start automatically and power their MCCs. @@ are automatically started to power their associated MCCs
S Batteries supply uninterrupted power until the SDGs come online. @@ The batteries supply uninterrupted power to the inverters and UPS panelboard loads until the SDGs come online

S Charger DC overvoltage protection shuts the charger down before inverter overvoltage protection acts. @@ shuts down the charger automatically before the inverter overvoltage protection is activated

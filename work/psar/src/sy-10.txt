# SY-10 | Water Pool Fuel Handling System | system | FHP
range: 7.3-1 7.3-16
sec: Sec. 7.3.1, pp. 7.3-1 to 7.3-16
intro: The FHP is where spent fuel ends up. It takes assemblies out of sodium, cleans them, and stores them under water with passive cooling as the safety function.

# SY-18 | Nuclear Instrumentation System | system | XIS
range: 7.6-36 7.6-47
sec: Sec. 7.6.4, pp. 7.6-36 to 7.6-47
intro: The XIS measures neutron leakage outside the core and turns it into a power signal for the RPS and NIC. It is analog, with one detector per division.

# SY-13 | Gaseous Radwaste Processing System | system | RWG
range: 7.4-1 7.4-9
sec: Sec. 7.4.1, pp. 7.4-1 to 7.4-9
intro: The RWG delays and filters waste gas until short-lived isotopes decay, then sends a monitored release to the plant stack.

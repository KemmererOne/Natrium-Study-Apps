intro: The NFP is four systems. Three are conventional fire protection. The fourth, NNA, deals with sodium, and sodium buildings are kept free of fire water.

== Sodium leak strategy | 3,4
S NNA detects leaked sodium, collects it, and drains it to catch pans, while giving operators early notice. @@ giving operators early notification of a leak so they can take necessary action
S The overall strategy uses passive features and adds active ones only where necessary. @@ The overall sodium fire strategy is to use passive prevention and mitigation features
T Feature | Description
R Cell | Room with limited leak rate and known oxygen volume, either inerted or de facto inerted after a leak @@ Cells are rooms with a limited leak rate and known oxygen volume
R Guard pipe | Fully redundant pipe that acts as a full secondary pressure boundary @@ Guard pipes are fully redundant pipes designed to the same conditions as the protected pipe
R Guard enclosure | Like a guard pipe, with a drainpipe to a collection location @@ Guard enclosures act similarly to guard pipes
R Clamshell | Guard enclosure placed where piping inspections are expected @@ A clamshell is a form of guard enclosure placed specifically where piping inspections are expected
R Catch pan | Steel-lined volume that permanently retains a design-basis leak volume @@ Catch pans are a steel-lined volume designed to permanently retain design-basis sodium leak volume
R Suppression deck | Applied to catch pans where oxygen volume cannot be limited @@ Suppression decks will be applied to catch pans where the environmental oxygen volume cannot be limited
S Primary SPS cells in the RAB are inerted. IHT cells in the RAB are not. @@ inerted cells for portions of the primary SPS within the RAB and non-inerted cells for portions of the IHT
S Intermediate sodium leaks in the HAA must be fully contained and removed from the HAA. @@ fully contain sodium leakage from the intermediate sodium SSCs within the HAA

K What is a clamshell? | A guard enclosure placed where inspections are expected | A catch pan lid | A fire damper | An argon valve | 0 | A clamshell is a form of guard enclosure placed where piping inspections are expected. @@ A clamshell is a form of guard enclosure placed specifically where piping inspections are expected

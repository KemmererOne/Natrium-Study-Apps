# SY-22 | Anticipatory Automatic Seismic Trip System | system | AST
range: 7.6-75 7.6-80
sec: Sec. 7.6.8, pp. 7.6-75 to 7.6-80
intro: The AST scrams the reactor before the peak seismic wave arrives. It is NSRST, separate from RPS logic, and acts directly on the reactor trip breakers.

== Purpose and interfaces | 1,2
S The AST measures ground motion, applies processing and logic, and sends actuation signals to the RTBs through the RPS interface device. @@ measures ground motion, applies required signal processing and logic
S It is used in seismic beyond design basis events greater than the SSE, to scram before the peak wave reaches the facility. @@ to scram the reactor before the peak seismic wave reaches the facility
S The RTB interface device is downstream of the RPS logic, where an analog relay matrix isolates the RTB power supply control. @@ is located downstream of the RPS logic process

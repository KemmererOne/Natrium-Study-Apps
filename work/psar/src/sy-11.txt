# SY-11 | Ex-Vessel Fuel Handling System | system | FHE
range: 7.3-17 7.3-39
sec: Sec. 7.3.2, pp. 7.3-17 to 7.3-39
intro: The FHE carries core assemblies between the reactor, the EVST, and the pool. Every step stays inert and shielded, and every carrier can cool fuel without power.

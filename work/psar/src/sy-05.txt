# SY-05 | Reactor Air Cooling System | system | RAC
range: 7.2-1 7.2-12
sec: Sec. 7.2.1, pp. 7.2-1 to 7.2-12
intro: RAC is the always-on passive heat removal path. Air rises past the guard vessel and leaves through tall stacks. There is nothing to start and nothing to stop.

== Purpose | 1

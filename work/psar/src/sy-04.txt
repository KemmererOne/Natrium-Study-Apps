# SY-04 | Intermediate Heat Transport System | system | IHT
range: 7.1-75 7.1-85 7.1-63 7.1-63
sec: Sec. 7.1.4, pp. 7.1-75 to 7.1-85
intro: The IHT carries heat from the IHXs to the salt system at power and to the air heat exchangers at low power and shutdown. It has two loops, and either pumped or natural circulation flow.

== Purpose | 1
S The IHT transports heat to the NSS for electrical power generation and energy storage during normal operation. @@ transports heat from the primary coolant to the Nuclear Island Salt System (NSS)
S It transports heat to the IAC for rejection to the atmosphere during start-up and shutdown, as the normal and preferred means of reactor cooling. @@ as the normal and preferred means of providing reactor cooling
S On loss of pumped flow, the IHT provides natural circulation for decay heat removal through the IAC AHXs. @@ In the event of a loss of pumped flow
K What is the normal and preferred means of reactor cooling during start-up and shutdown? | RAC | Transport of heat through the IHT to the IAC | The Energy Island | SPS cooling | 1 | IHT to IAC is the normal and preferred means. @@ as the normal and preferred means of providing reactor cooling

== Components and boundaries | 2,3
S Each of the two loops has an ISP, an array of SHXs, and interconnecting piping. Each loop connects to one IHX and one IAC AHX. @@ Each loop includes an Intermediate Sodium Pump (ISP), an array of Sodium-Salt Heat Exchangers
S Supporting equipment includes expansion tanks and a drain subsystem. @@ The IHT supporting equipment includes expansion tanks and a drain subsystem
S Loop piping and components are in the Reactor Auxiliary Building, except piping in the Reactor Building to the IHX nozzles and yard piping to the AHXs. @@ The system loop piping and components are located within the Reactor Auxiliary Building
S The expansion tank interfaces with the SCG for an inert cover gas and overpressure protection, and with the SPS for sodium processing. @@ to prevent sodium-air interaction and to provide IHT over-pressure protection
S IHT piping in the HAA has a continuous leak guard as a secondary barrier. @@ includes a continuous leak guard enclosing the pipe as a secondary barrier

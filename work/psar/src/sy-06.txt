# SY-06 | Intermediate Air Cooling System | system | IAC
range: 7.2-13 7.2-21
sec: Sec. 7.2.2, pp. 7.2-13 to 7.2-21
intro: IAC is the normal way to reject reactor heat at low power and shutdown. Two trains of air heat exchangers sit in the yard, with three modes from fully powered to fully passive.

== Purpose | 1
S IAC transfers heat from the intermediate sodium to the atmosphere. @@ transfers heat from the intermediate sodium (described in Section 7.1.4) to the atmosphere
S IAC is the primary and preferred means of reactor heat removal during low power and shutdown conditions. @@ is the primary and preferred means of reactor heat removal during low power and shutdown conditions
S It also supports start-up and shutdown transitions to and from NSS cooling. @@ for transition to and from Nuclear Island Salt System cooling
K When is IAC the primary and preferred means of reactor heat removal? | Full power | Low power and shutdown conditions | Only in accidents | Only during refueling | 1 | IAC is primary and preferred during low power and shutdown. @@ is the primary and preferred means of reactor heat removal during low power and shutdown conditions

== Components and boundaries | 2,3
S IAC has two equal capacity trains. Each has an AHX, air stack, air blower, dampers, and air heater in an ASE structure. @@ consists of two equal capacity trains
S The two ASEs are in the yard on either side of the RAB. @@ The two ASEs are located in the yard area on either side of the Reactor Auxiliary Building
S An AHX is in series within each intermediate loop. Sodium is on the tube side and air on the shell side. @@ Intermediate sodium flows on the tube-side of the AHX with airflow on the shell-side

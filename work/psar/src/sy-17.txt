# SY-17 | Reactor Protection System | system | RPS
range: 7.6-18 7.6-35
sec: Sec. 7.6.3, pp. 7.6-18 to 7.6-35
intro: The RPS is the safety-related logic that scrams the reactor and trips pumps. Four divisions, two-out-of-four voting, and de-energize to trip are the three ideas to master.

== De-energize to trip | 4,6,8
S In normal operation, the RPS keeps voltage on the RTB undervoltage coils, which holds RTBs closed. @@ the RPS maintains voltage on the RTB undervoltage coils to keep RTBs in the closed position
S Closed RTBs keep power on CRD solenoid valve coils, which keep argon pressure to hold the rods. @@ maintaining power to the CRD solenoid operated valve coils
S On loss of incoming power, RTBs open, CRD argon vents, and rods drop by gravity. @@ venting CRD argon and allowing rods to drop fully into the core via gravity
S ESF circuits also de-energize to trip. On loss of power, relay contacts open and pump breakers trip. @@ tripping the pump breakers, and stopping pump rotation
S On a critical fault found by self-diagnostics, the system places all outputs in the safe, tripped state. @@ the system places all outputs to the safe (tripped) state
S Range-checking detects loss of power at a transmitting system, and the RPS places the associated outputs in the safe state. @@ loss of power at the transmitting system is detected by RPS range-checking
S Once started, the sequence of protective actions continues to completion. Deliberate operator action is required to return the RPS to normal. @@ Deliberate operator action is required to return the RPS to normal
K RPS incoming power is lost. What happens? | Nothing | RTBs open and rods drop by gravity | Motors drive rods out | RTBs stay closed | 1 | The circuit is de-energize to trip. @@ venting CRD argon and allowing rods to drop fully into the core via gravity
K Self-diagnostics find a critical fault. What does the RPS do? | Alarms only | Places all outputs in the safe state | Reboots | Bypasses the division | 1 | The system places all outputs to the safe, tripped state. @@ the system places all outputs to the safe (tripped) state
K What is needed to return the RPS to normal after an actuation? | Time delay | Deliberate operator action | Parameter below setpoint | Reset by the NIC | 1 | Deliberate operator action is required. @@ Deliberate operator action is required to return the RPS to normal

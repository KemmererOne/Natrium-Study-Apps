# SY-07 | Sodium Cover Gas System | system | SCG
range: 7.2-22 7.2-40
sec: Sec. 7.2.3, pp. 7.2-22 to 7.2-40
intro: The SCG supplies inert argon across the plant. It keeps air out, relieves overpressure, watches for leaks, and finds failed fuel.

== Purpose and scope | 1,2
S The SCG controls, monitors, and supplies inert argon gas in the RXB, RAB, and FHB. @@ controls, monitors, and supplies inert argon gas to various systems and components throughout
S It comprises the Primary SCG and the Intermediate SCG. @@ The SCG comprises the Primary SCG and the Intermediate SCG
S Both receive purified argon from the NGA, with design conditions of 250 psig and 110 F. Local accumulators add inventory if NGA supply is disrupted. @@ Local argon accumulators are located directly downstream of the NGA supply
S SCG piping that penetrates the RES, up to and including the first isolation valve, is part of the primary coolant boundary. @@ SCG piping that penetrates the RES, up to and including the first isolation valve

== Detecting an RV leak | 5,8
S Annulus argon is monitored continuously for oxygen content, radioactivity, and filter pressure drop. @@ The argon gas is monitored continuously during operation for oxygen content, radioactivity
T Indicator | Change on an RV leak | Why
R Oxygen | Drops | Sodium or vapor oxidizes @@ the diatomic oxygen content is expected to drop
R Activity | May increase | Argon-41, Neon-23, fission products, or sodium radioisotopes enter the annulus @@ activity in the annulus may increase
R Filter pressure drop | Increases | Sodium aerosols or oxide accumulate @@ an increase in pressure drop across a filter may also indicate a leak
K Which trend in the RV-GV annulus gas indicates an RV sodium leak? | Oxygen rising | Oxygen dropping | Filter pressure drop falling | Argon flow rising | 1 | Oxygen drops as sodium or sodium vapor oxidizes. @@ the diatomic oxygen content is expected to drop

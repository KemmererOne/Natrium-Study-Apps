## New items

### Quiz (PSAR: Significant Support Systems and Components): What is the primary purpose of the Nuclear Island Major Maintenance Equipment (NME) as described in the PSAR?

- **Answer:** Install and remove the IVTM and FTL to support refueling operations
- **Distractors:** Store irradiated core assemblies in a water-cooled pool; Distribute and store argon as the cover gas for sodium-containing systems; Provide illumination in NI plant areas during a station blackout
- **Explanation:** The PSAR says the NME is used for the installation and removal of the IVTM and FTL to support refueling operations.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.3.19, p. 1.1-18 (PDF page 53)
- **Quote:** "Nuclear Island Major Maintenance Equipment (NME) is used for the installation and removal of the IVTM and FTL to support refueling operations."

### Card (PSAR: Significant Support Systems and Components): What is the primary purpose of the Nuclear Island Major Maintenance Equipment (NME)?

- **Answer:** Install and remove the IVTM and FTL to support refueling operations.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.3.19, p. 1.1-18 (PDF page 53)
- **Quote:** "Nuclear Island Major Maintenance Equipment (NME) is used for the installation and removal of the IVTM and FTL to support refueling operations."

### Card (PSAR: Significant Support Systems and Components): Why does the NME provide an inert environment?

- **Answer:** To prevent excess air ingress into the primary cover gas space and excess leakage into the Reactor Building during reactor operating mode 4.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 1.1.4.3.19, p. 1.1-18 (PDF page 53)
- **Quote:** "The NME is designed to provide an inert environment to prevent excess air ingress into the primary cover gas space and prevent excess leakage into the Reactor Building during reactor operating mode 4."

### Quiz (PSAR: Buildings and Structures): What does the Reactor Building (RXB) superstructure structurally support?

- **Answer:** A single-failure proof overhead bridge crane
- **Distractors:** The Reactor Air Cooling System stacks; The reactor vessel and guard vessel; The Ex-Vessel Storage Tank
- **Explanation:** The RXB superstructure provides structural support for a single-failure proof overhead bridge crane for moving equipment and other heavy loads.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.1.2, p. 7.8-7 (PDF page 1568)
- **Quote:** "The RXB superstructure provides structural support for a single-failure proof overhead bridge crane for movement of equipment and other heavy loads for maintenance within the facility"

### Card (PSAR: Buildings and Structures): What is the Reactor Building (RXB) superstructure?

- **Answer:** An above-grade steel framed structure supported on a concrete foundation independent from the RXB substructure.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.8.1.2, p. 7.8-7 (PDF page 1568)
- **Quote:** "The RXB superstructure is an above-grade steel framed structure supported on concrete foundation independent from the RXB substructure."

### Card (PSAR: Ancillary Systems): What do guard pipes do in the event of a sodium leak?

- **Answer:** They act as a full secondary pressure boundary, designed to the same conditions as the protected system pipe.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 8.2.1.3, p. 8.2-2 (PDF page 1615)
- **Quote:** "Guard pipes act as a full secondary pressure boundary in the event of a leak and are designed to the same conditions as the protected system pipe."

### Card (PSAR: Ancillary Systems): What is a sodium catch pan?

- **Answer:** A steel-lined volume designed to retain the design-basis sodium leak volume plus margin.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 8.2.1.6, p. 8.2-2 (PDF page 1615)
- **Quote:** "Catch pans are a steel-lined volume designed to retain the design-basis sodium leak volume plus margin."

### Card (PSAR: Ancillary Systems): What is a clamshell in the sodium leak design?

- **Answer:** A form of guard enclosure placed specifically where piping inspections are expected to be needed.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-15 (PDF page 1466)
- **Quote:** "A clamshell is a form of guard enclosure placed specifically where piping inspections are expected to be needed."

### Card (PSAR: Ancillary Systems): What is a cell in the sodium fire protection design?

- **Answer:** A room with a limited leak rate and known oxygen volume that is either actively inerted or becomes de facto inerted once a sodium leak consumes the limited available oxygen.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-15 (PDF page 1466)
- **Quote:** "Cells are rooms with a limited leak rate and known oxygen volume which is either actively inerted or becomes de facto inerted upon consumption of the limited available oxygen after a sodium leak."

### Card (PSAR: Ancillary Systems): What provides supplemental sodium leak detection in non-inerted fire areas?

- **Answer:** Conventional fire detection.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 8.2.1.8, p. 8.2-3 (PDF page 1616)
- **Quote:** "Conventional fire detection installed in non-inerted fire areas provides supplemental sodium leak detection."

### Quiz (PSAR: Ancillary Systems): Besides mitigating sodium-air and sodium-concrete reactions, what do catch pans provide?

- **Answer:** A location where leaked sodium can safely cool
- **Distractors:** A path that returns leaked sodium to the primary system; A sealed volume that keeps leaked sodium molten; A source of inert gas for the surrounding room
- **Explanation:** Catch pans mitigate sodium-air and sodium-concrete reactions and provide a location where leaked sodium can safely cool.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.5.2.3.4, p. 7.5-16 (PDF page 1467)
- **Quote:** "Catch pans mitigate the effects of sodium-air and sodium-concrete reactions and provide a location where leaked sodium can safely cool."

### Quiz (PSAR: Ancillary Systems): The Natrium sodium fire protection strategy uses which elements?

- **Answer:** Leak prevention, detection, and passive mitigation
- **Distractors:** Leak prevention, water suppression, and evacuation; Leak detection, active flooding, and forced cooling; Passive mitigation, foam suppression, and venting
- **Explanation:** The strategy is based on a defense-in-depth design approach and uses leak prevention, detection, and passive mitigation.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 8.2.2, p. 8.2-3 (PDF page 1616)
- **Quote:** "The sodium fire protection strategy is based on a defense-in-depth fire protection design approach and utilizes leak prevention, detection and passive mitigation."

### Quiz (PSAR: Ancillary Systems): Why may sodium-air reactions occur within guard enclosures?

- **Answer:** They are not inerted
- **Distractors:** They are not steel lined; They are held above atmospheric pressure; They have no drain lines
- **Explanation:** Sodium-air reactions may occur within guard enclosures because they are not inerted.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 8.2.2.2, p. 8.2-4 (PDF page 1617)
- **Quote:** "Sodium-air reactions may occur within guard enclosures as they are not inerted."

### Quiz (PSAR: Ancillary Systems): In a process cell with an inerted environment, what is true of the catch pan?

- **Answer:** It does not have a suppression deck
- **Distractors:** It always has a suppression deck; It has no drain connection; It is replaced by a guard pipe
- **Explanation:** For process cells with an inerted environment, the catch pan does not have a suppression deck.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 8.2.2.3, p. 8.2-4 (PDF page 1617)
- **Quote:** "For process cells which have an inerted environment, the catch pan does not have a suppression deck."

### Quiz (PSAR: Ancillary Systems): Why is no suppression deck provided on catch pans for operational leakage?

- **Answer:** Significant sodium-air reaction is not expected
- **Distractors:** The catch pan is located inside an inerted cell; Leaked sodium drains away to a process cell; The surrounding berm has a suppression deck
- **Explanation:** Operational leakage catch pans have no suppression deck since significant sodium-air reaction is not expected.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 8.2.2.1, p. 8.2-4 (PDF page 1617)
- **Quote:** "No suppression deck is provided since significant sodium-air reaction is not expected."

### Card (PSAR: Reactor Auxiliary Systems): What does the plugging temperature indicator (PTI) provide in the Sodium Processing System?

- **Answer:** Continuous monitoring of the plugging temperature of the sodium, giving real-time indication of the overall sodium chemistry.
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 7.2.4.3.1, p. 7.2-51 (PDF page 1369)
- **Quote:** "The PTI will use this correlation to continuously monitor the plugging temperature of the sodium, providing real-time indication of the overall sodium chemistry."

### Card (PSAR: Fuel Handling Systems): Where are new core assemblies placed after removal from the shipping container?

- **Answer:** In the assembly receipt inspection stand (ARIS).
- **Source:** PSAR Rev 1, ML25276A288, Sec. 3.14.2.5, p. 3.14-6 (PDF page 1078)
- **Quote:** "The core assemblies will be removed from the shipping container and placed in the assembly receipt inspection stand (ARIS)."
